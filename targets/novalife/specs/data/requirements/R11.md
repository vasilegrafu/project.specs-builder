---
id: R11
name: Not for emergencies
priority: must
serves: G1
---

The website shall say, on every page and on booking, that it is not for medical emergencies, and give the emergency number to call instead.

## Accepted when

- R11.1 Given any page, when it opens, then it says that the website is not for medical emergencies, and to call 911 in one.
- R11.2 Given booking, when it opens, then it says, above the form, that a booked appointment is not emergency care and that an emergency is a call to that number.
