---
id: R16
name: Patient account
priority: must
serves: G1, G2
---

When a visitor creates an account with their first and last name, date of birth, email, phone and a password, the website shall open the account and book with its details from then on.

## Accepted when

- R16.1 Given a visitor with no account, when they give their first and last name, date of birth, email, phone and a password and create the account, then it opens, and booking fills in their details.
- R16.2 Given an email that already has an account, when a visitor creates an account with it, then the website refuses, says that the email has an account, and offers to sign in or to reset the password.
- R16.3 Given a detail missing, or a date of birth in the future, when a visitor creates the account, then the website refuses and says which detail and why, keeping everything else written.
- R16.4 Given a patient with an account, when they sign in with their email and password, then their account opens.
- R16.5 Given a wrong password, or an email with no account, when someone signs in, then sign-in is refused with one message that does not say which of the two was wrong.
- R16.6 Given a patient who has forgotten their password, when they ask to reset it, then the website sends a link to their email that lets them set a new one.
