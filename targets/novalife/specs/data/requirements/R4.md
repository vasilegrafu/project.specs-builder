---
id: R4
name: The vision and the ambition, with their limit
priority: must
serves: G2
---

The website shall present NovaLife's vision and its long-term ambition of healthy longevity, and say wherever it does so that immortality is an aspirational idea, not a current medical capability or a promise the clinic makes.

## Accepted when

- R4.1 Given the About page, when it opens, then it shows the vision "The Future of Health", the mission, and the ambition to push the boundaries of healthy human longevity through evidence-based prevention, early detection and better management of established health risks.
- R4.2 Given any page that speaks of longevity or of the ambition, when it opens, then it says that immortality is an aspirational idea, not a current medical capability or a promise the clinic makes.
- R4.3 Given any page of the website, when it is read, then no sentence on it promises a longer life, a cure or a result of care.
