---
id: R7
name: The price of each appointment type
priority: should
serves: G1
---

The website shall show the price of each appointment type on its service's page and in booking before anything is booked.

## Accepted when

- R7.1 Given a service's page, when it opens, then it shows each of the service's appointment types with its price in US dollars.
- R7.2 Given booking, when an appointment type is chosen, then its price shows before the visitor books.
- R7.3 Given an appointment type's price has changed, when its service's page or booking opens, then the new price shows.
- R7.4 Given any service's page, when it opens, then it says that patients pay the clinic themselves, and that the clinic does not bill health insurance.
