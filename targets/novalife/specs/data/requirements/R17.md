---
id: R17
name: My bookings
priority: must
serves: G1
---

While a patient is signed in, the website shall show them their bookings, those to come and those past, and let them cancel one from there (R15).

## Accepted when

- R17.1 Given a signed-in patient with a booking on November 20 and one on October 2, when they open My bookings on October 17, then November 20 shows under those to come and October 2 under those past.
- R17.2 Given a signed-in patient with no booking, when they open My bookings, then it says they have none, and offers booking.
- R17.3 Given a booking to come, when the patient cancels it from My bookings, then it is cancelled as R15 says.
- R17.4 Given another patient's booking, when this patient is signed in, then it never shows to them.
