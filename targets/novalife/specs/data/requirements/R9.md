The website shall answer, on one page, how to become a patient, what happens at the first visit, and what to bring to it.

## Accepted when

- R9.1 Given the page for new patients, when it opens, then it answers how to become a patient, what happens at the first visit, and what to bring to it.
- R9.2 Given an answer on that page that concerns a service, when it is read, then it leads to that service's page (R2).
