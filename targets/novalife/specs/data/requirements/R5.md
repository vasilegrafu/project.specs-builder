---
id: R5
name: Opening hours, address and how to reach the clinic
priority: must
serves: G1
---

The website shall show the clinic's opening hours, its address, how to get there, its phone number and its email on a Contact page reached from every page.

## Accepted when

- R5.1 Given any page, when Contact is chosen, then the Contact page opens.
- R5.2 Given the Contact page, when it opens, then it shows the hours of each day of the week, the address with a map of where it is, how to get there, the phone with the hours it is answered, and the email.
- R5.3 Given the clinic's hours have changed, when the Contact page opens, then it shows the new hours.
