---
id: R14
name: Staff set each doctor's free times
priority: must
serves: G1, G2
---

The website shall let the staff set the days and hours each doctor sees patients -- an administrator or the front desk for any doctor, a doctor for themselves (R18) -- and offer in booking only those hours that are not yet booked.

## Why

A visitor books a doctor's free time (D1), so booking can offer only the hours the staff have set. The clinic chose to let doctors set their own hours and the front desk any doctor's, rather than the front desk alone (D10).

## Accepted when

- R14.1 Given a doctor set to see patients on Mondays from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM, when a visitor books a 30-minute appointment type with that doctor, then booking offers every half hour from 9:00 to 12:30 on Mondays, less the times already booked, one appointment starting as the one before it ends (R6).
- R14.2 Given a doctor marked away on December 24, when a visitor books with that doctor, then booking offers no time on December 24.
- R14.3 Given a day on which a doctor already has bookings, when the doctor is marked away that day, then the website names those bookings, and saves the change only once each is moved or cancelled.
