---
id: R2
name: A page for each of the four services
priority: must
serves: G1, G2
---

The website shall give each of NovaLife's four services -- general medical care; prevention and early detection; longevity and healthy aging; high-tech, personalized healthcare -- a page of its own that says what the service covers, who it is for and how to start it.

## Accepted when

- R2.1 Given the general medical care page, when it opens, then it names consultations, medical assessments, diagnosis, treatment, referrals and follow-up by qualified healthcare professionals.
- R2.2 Given the prevention and early detection page, when it opens, then it names screening, check-ups, diagnostic tests and regular health reviews.
- R2.3 Given the longevity and healthy aging page, when it opens, then it names maintaining physical and cognitive health, reducing preventable risks and supporting healthy aging over the long term.
- R2.4 Given the high-tech, personalized healthcare page, when it opens, then it names digital health records, longitudinal health data, AI-assisted workflows and monitoring tools, and says that technology supports the clinicians and does not replace their judgment.
- R2.5 Given any of the four pages, when it opens, then it says who the service is for, and offers booking with that service's appointment types (R6).
