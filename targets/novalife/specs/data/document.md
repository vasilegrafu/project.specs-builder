NovaLife Clinic's website presents the clinic -- its vision, its four services and how it differs from a traditional practice -- to the people who might become its patients.
It gives them, in one place, every practical thing they need to choose the clinic and to get in touch with it.
It is the clinic's marketing and its front door.

## Problem

NovaLife Clinic is a general medical clinic built around prevention, personalized care and healthy longevity, which its concept sets against the traditional, reactive approach.
Without a place that says what it offers, how it differs and how to become a patient, the people it is for cannot find out, and cannot choose it.
The clinic is not open yet: the website opens with it, so it is how people first learn of NovaLife, and nobody can find out about it or reach it any other way today, 2026-10-20.

## Users

The visitor: someone considering NovaLife for their own health -- for general care, for a check-up or screening, or for healthy aging -- who has not yet been in touch with the clinic.
The patient: someone with an account, who signs in to book, to see their bookings and to cancel one, and comes back for the hours, the address or how to reach the clinic.
The clinic: NovaLife's staff, who sign in to the staff area in one of three roles -- the administrator, who keeps everything the website shows current and the staff accounts; the front desk, who books and cancels on the website for a person who phones and sets the doctors' free times; and the doctors, who set their own.

## Non-goals

- A patient portal: a patient's account holds their details and their bookings, and nothing of their care -- health records, results, monitoring data and messages with a clinician belong to the clinic's care, not to this website.
- Medical advice: the website informs and does not assess symptoms or recommend care, because technology supports the clinicians and does not replace their judgment.
- Paying online: the website takes no payment.
- A second way to book, for the clinic's staff: a person who phones is booked by the staff on the website itself, so that every booking is made in one place.
