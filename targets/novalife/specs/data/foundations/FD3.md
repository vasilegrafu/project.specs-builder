name: Density and spacing
---

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| the spacing scale | 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64px: inside a control, between controls, between groups, between parts of a page |
| the height of a control | 44px for a button or a field, 40px for a free time, 32px for a small button |
| a row of a table | 12px above and below its text |
| the space between parts of a page | 32px; 64px above and below the home page's opening |
