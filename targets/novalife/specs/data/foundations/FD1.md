name: Colours and what they mean
---

| Colour | Value | Means | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| swatch accent | --color-accent, NovaLife teal | what can be pressed or followed: act here | primary buttons, links, the chosen free time, prices |
| swatch primary-soft | --color-primary-soft, pale teal | a quiet ground for what belongs to the action | the home page's opening, a chosen card, a slot under the pointer |
| swatch warm | --color-warm, coral | warmth and optimism; decoration only | the mark before the name, the rule under the opening, the top of a service's card, the rule over the footer |
| swatch ink | --color-ink, deep ink | text, and the staff area | text, the footer, the staff header |
| swatch muted | --color-muted | what is secondary | labels of facts, lengths, table heads |
| swatch line | --color-line | a border | cards, fields, tables |
| swatch bg | --color-bg | the page | behind everything |
| swatch surface | --color-surface | a surface | the header, cards, fields, dialogs |
| swatch good | --color-good | done | a message that something was booked, saved or cancelled |
| swatch bad | --color-bad | refused or failed | a message that something was refused or failed, a field in error |
| swatch warn | --color-warn on --color-warn-soft | take care | the notice that booking is not for emergencies |
| swatch primary-hover | --color-primary-hover | under the pointer | primary buttons and links under the pointer |
| swatch warm-soft | --color-warm-soft | warmth, as a ground | the band under the home page's opening |
| swatch good-soft | --color-good-soft | the ground of done | a message that something was booked, saved or cancelled |
| swatch bad-soft | --color-bad-soft | the ground of a refusal | a message that something was refused or failed |
| swatch on-dark-muted | --color-on-dark-muted | what is secondary, on the dark ground | the footer's small print, the staff header's role |
| swatch backdrop | --color-backdrop | the page behind a dialog | behind every dialog |

Coral is never text and never the only sign of anything: on white it is 2.6 to 1.
