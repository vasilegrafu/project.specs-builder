---
id: FD10
name: Accessibility
---

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| contrast | text at least 4.5 to 1: ink on white 15 to 1, teal on white 6.3 to 1, white on teal 6.3 to 1, muted on white 6 to 1 |
| focus | a 2px teal ring, 2px outside whatever has the focus, on everything that takes it |
| keyboard | everything reachable and usable by the keyboard alone, in reading order |
| a screen reader | a message is announced when it appears; a free time is a toggle button, pressed or not; a picture is named for what it shows |
| size | every control at least 44px high |
