Which privacy law applies to what booking keeps, in New York, and what does it require of booking and of the privacy notice (R6, R10)? Background read on 2026-10-20, for the adviser to confirm, not settled: HIPAA binds a provider that sends claims or other standard transactions electronically, so a clinic paid only by its patients may fall outside it unless it does so for anything, e-prescribing or a laboratory included; New York's SHIELD Act asks for reasonable security of the private information it keeps and notice of a breach, and was read as covering medical information since 2025; a New York health information privacy act was vetoed in December 2025 and a revised bill was pending in 2026; tracking scripts on a booking page are where federal guidance has been strictest.

## Why

booking takes a person's details for a medical appointment, and the law decides how they are kept, which tools the website may use on the booking pages, and what the notice must say
