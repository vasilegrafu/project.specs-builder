FD10 says every control is at least 44 pixels high; FD3 says 44 for a button or a field, 40 for a free time and 32 for a small button; and the links of the header and the footer are drawn 24 high. Which holds?

## Why

It decides how every link, free time and small button is built, and whether the prototypes change.
