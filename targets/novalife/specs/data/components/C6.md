What a person presses to do something: book, save, sign in, cancel. One primary button per screen or part, for the step that moves the work on; secondary for the other choices; small inside a row of a table or a list.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| primary | the main step of a screen or a part |
| primary, under the pointer | a darker teal: it can be pressed |
| primary, with the keyboard's focus | a teal ring two pixels outside it |
| primary, disabled | while what it started is under way, and it cannot be pressed twice |
| secondary | a choice beside the primary one |
| small | in a row of a table or a list |

## Rules

- At least 44 pixels high, 32 when small, and never narrower than its words.
- It says the verb, in sentence case: Book, Save, Cancel -- never OK.
- A link that only leads somewhere stays a link; a button does something.
- Its look is the proposal of the foundations, 2026-10-20, to be changed with them. (foundations)
