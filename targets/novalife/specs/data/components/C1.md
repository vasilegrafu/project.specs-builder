## Rules

- The same on every public screen, in the same order, white, held at the top of the window as the page scrolls.
- Nothing in it is a filled button: every item is set in the menu's own words and size, and Register is only outlined, so that the row reads as one menu.
- Booking is not in it: it is offered where a visitor decides to book -- the home page's opening and foot, each appointment type, About, New patients and My bookings. (G1, G2)
- Part 8, the booking button it once held, is retired and its number not used again.
- At a phone's width the five pages move to a row of their own beneath, which scrolls sideways; the clinic's name, Sign in and Register stay in view.
- Its look is the proposal of the foundations, 2026-10-20, to be changed with them. (foundations)
