The notice above a booking form that a booked appointment is not emergency care, and that an emergency is a call to 911 (R11). Use it at the top of each step of booking; elsewhere the footer's line, C2, is enough.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| default | above each step of booking |

## Rules

- It stands above the form, before anything a visitor fills in, and is never closed or hidden.
- Its words are fixed: Not for emergencies. A booked appointment is not emergency care. In a medical emergency, call 911. (R11)
- Amber, the colour of a warning: never the red of a refusal, since nothing has gone wrong.
- A screen reader reads it as a note, before the form.
- Its look is the proposal of the foundations, 2026-10-20, to be changed with them. (foundations)
