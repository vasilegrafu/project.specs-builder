What the website tells a person about what just happened or what is so: a refusal, a failure, a success, or a plain fact such as a list with nothing in it. A refusal under one field is a line of red text beneath it.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| refused or failed | something was refused or failed: red, and announced |
| done | something was done: green, and announced |
| a fact | nothing went wrong: plain |

## Rules

- It says what happened, what did not change, and what to do next, in that order.
- It stands above what it is about, at the top of the form or the list, and a screen reader is told of it when it appears.
- Colour is never the only sign: the words say whether it failed or was done.
- Its look is the proposal of the foundations, 2026-10-20, to be changed with them. (foundations)
