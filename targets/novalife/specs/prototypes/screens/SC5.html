Becoming a patient

How to become a patient

Book your first appointment online: choose a service and an appointment type, then a free time, and create your account as you book. No referral or earlier consultation is needed.

See the services

What happens at the first visit

Your doctor talks with you about your health, your history and what you want from your care, examines you where it is needed, and agrees with you what comes next.

What to bring

A photo ID, a list of the medicines you take, and any recent results or letters from other doctors.
