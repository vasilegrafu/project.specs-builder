A member of staff ends having read everything customers have said, and signed out.

## Steps

1. A member of staff opens the staff address and signs in. SC5, R4
2. A wrong password is refused, and repeated failures are blocked for a time. SC5, R4
3. They read the feedback, the newest first, with each date and time. SC6, R3, R2
4. They see each rating, and feedback rated 1 or 2 stars is marked Unhappy. SC6, R6, R10
5. They see the customer's address where one was left, and reply from their own email. SC6, R5
6. They see which feedback may be published, and since when. SC6, R9, R22
7. With nothing yet, the list says so. SC6, R3
8. They sign out. SC6, SC5, R23
