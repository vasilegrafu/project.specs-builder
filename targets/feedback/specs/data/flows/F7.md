A member of staff reads the month's numbers and sees whether things are better or worse.

## Steps

1. A member of staff signs in and opens the monthly figures. SC5, SC8, R4
2. They read how many pieces of feedback arrived each month. SC8, R8
3. They read each month's average rating; a month with no ratings shows none. SC8, R7
4. They read how many requests went out and how many customers opted out. SC8, R20
5. They read who opted out, and when. SC8, R22
