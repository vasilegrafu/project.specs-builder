---
id: F3
name: The request after delivery
trigger: an order's delivery becoming known, or 7 days after an order with no delivery known
serves: G1, G2, G3, G4, G5
passes: SC3, SC4
---

A customer who has their order is asked for feedback once, and no oftener than once a month.

## Steps

1. The shop platform says an order has been delivered. R13
2. Where no delivery can be known, the website waits until 7 days after the order. R18
3. A customer who opted out is skipped. R17
4. A customer who had a request in the past month is skipped. R14
5. A customer who has an email address is sent the request by email. SC3, R15
6. A customer with only a mobile number is sent it by text message. SC4, R15
7. A customer who cannot be reached is skipped, and nothing else changes. R13

## When it goes wrong

A request that cannot be delivered does not stop any other customer's request. R13
If the website is down when a request falls due, it sends it once it is back. R21
