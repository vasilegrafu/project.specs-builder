The customer ends with no more requests, and is still able to give feedback.

## Steps

1. The customer selects the Stop link in a request. SC3, SC4, R17
2. The page asks them to confirm, and they press Stop asking me. SC2, R17
3. A link the website did not give says so, and nothing changes. SC2
4. If the opt-out cannot be kept, the page says so and they can press again. SC2, R17
5. The website keeps the opt-out with its date, and says they won't be asked again. SC2, R22
6. They can still give feedback from the link on that page. SC2, SC1, R17
