The customer ends with their feedback received, and thanked for it.

## Steps

1. The customer opens the feedback page from a request, or by scanning the printed QR code. SC3, SC4, SC1, R13, R16
2. They pick 1 to 5 stars, write what they want to say, or both, with no account. SC1, R1, R6
3. If they want a reply, they leave an email address. SC1, R5
4. If the business may publish their words, they tick the permission. SC1, R9
5. They press Send. With everything empty, the page says there is nothing to send. SC1, R1
6. An address that does not look right is refused, and their words stay. SC1, R5
7. If the feedback cannot be kept, the page says it was not received, and their words stay to send again. SC1, R1
8. The website keeps it with the date and time, and says thank you. SC1, R2
9. Feedback rated 1 or 2 stars is emailed to every member of staff. SC10, R11
