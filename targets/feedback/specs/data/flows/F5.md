A member of staff hears of an unhappy customer as soon as the feedback arrives, and replies.

## Steps

1. Feedback rated 1 or 2 stars arrives, and every member of staff receives an email holding it. SC10, R11
2. They open the list from the email, signing in first where needed. SC10, SC5, SC6, R4
3. The feedback stands marked Unhappy. SC6, R10
4. They reply from their own email where the customer left an address; with none, they cannot reply. SC6, R5
