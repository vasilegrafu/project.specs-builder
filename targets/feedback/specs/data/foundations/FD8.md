name: Patterns
---

| Pattern | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A refused form | Says what is wrong above the form in C6, or under the field in C4, and keeps what was written. | That email address doesn't look right. |
| Something failed | Says so in C6, and offers a way to try again. | We couldn't load the feedback. Try again |
| What cannot be undone | A dialog says what will go, with a danger C3 that does it and a secondary C3 that keeps it; the focus starts on the one that keeps it. | Remove this feedback? |
| Nothing to show | A sentence in a dashed box. | No feedback yet. |
| Done | A tick, a title that says so, and a line. | Thank you |
| Dates and times | The day, the month in words; the time on 24 hours. | 3 March, 14:05 |
| A rating | The stars and the number together, always. | 2 of 5 |
