name: Colours and what they mean
---

| Colour | Value | Means | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| The page | #F7F8FA | the page's ground | behind every card and panel |
| The surface | #FFFFFF | what holds the content | cards, forms, panels, the header |
| A quiet ground | #EEF1F5 | nothing to act on | Not for publishing in C7, a text message |
| The words | #1B2430 | what is read | every heading and body text |
| Muted words | #566173 | said in passing | a date, a hint, a table's heads |
| An edge | #DDE2EA | a boundary | cards, panels, tables, the header's foot |
| A control's edge | #8A94A6 | can be used | a field, a secondary C3, an empty star |
| The accent | #2456C8 | can be pressed | a primary C3, links, the focus ring, the screen open in C2 |
| The accent, darker | #1B429C | under the pointer | a primary C3 and links under the pointer |
| A star | #B7791F | a star filled | C1 only |
| Bad news | #B42318 | unhappy, refused, or cannot be undone | C6, Unhappy in C7, a refused field in C4, a danger C3 |
| Its ground | #FEF3F2 | behind bad news | C6, Unhappy in C7 |
| Good news | #067647 | may be published, or done | May be published in C7, the tick on a done page |
| Its ground | #ECFDF3 | behind good news | May be published in C7, the tick on a done page |
