---
id: FD10
name: Accessibility
---

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| The standard | WCAG 2.2 level AA (Y6) |
| Contrast | every pair of text and ground at 4.5:1 or more, and a star and a control's edge at 3:1 or more on white |
| Focus | a 2px ring in the accent, #2456C8, 2px off the element, on every link, button and field |
| Keyboard | Tab reaches every control in reading order; the stars are one group of radio buttons, moved through with the arrow keys |
| A screen reader | C6 is an alert, announced when it appears; a rating is read as its number out of 5 |
| Colour | never alone: Unhappy says its word, and a rating its number |
