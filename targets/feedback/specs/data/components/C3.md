What a person presses: to send, to sign in, to confirm or to keep.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| primary | the one main action of a screen |
| secondary | an action beside the main one |
| danger | confirming what cannot be undone |
| quiet danger | starting something that cannot be undone, from a list |
| under the pointer | the pointer is over it |
| keyboard focus | the focus is on it |
| busy | it has been pressed, and cannot be pressed again |

## Rules

One primary button on a screen at most. It says what it does. Danger is only for what cannot be undone, and always beside a way to keep it. The quiet danger button is for Remove in a list.
