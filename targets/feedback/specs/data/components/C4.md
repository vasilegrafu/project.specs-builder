---
id: C4
name: Text field
---

Where a person types: their feedback, an email address, a password.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| default | waiting for words |
| under the pointer | the pointer is over it |
| keyboard focus | the focus is on it |
| refused | what was typed cannot be accepted |

## Rules

Always has a label above it. A refused field keeps what was typed, and says why beneath it.
