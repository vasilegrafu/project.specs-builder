The five stars a customer picks from, and staff and emails read back.

## Parts

| # | Part | Kind | What it shows and does | States | Heard as | Component | Shows | About | Asks | Leads to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The stars | group | Five stars, those up to the rating filled, with the number in words beside them. |  | Rating, 2 out of 5 stars |  | R6 |  |  |  |

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| none picked | the customer has not picked yet, or has picked no rating |
| picked | the customer has picked a number of stars |
| read only | staff read a rating; it cannot be changed |
| under the pointer | the pointer is over a star |
| keyboard focus | the focus is on the stars |

## Rules

One to five stars and nothing else. The number is always written beside the stars, so that a rating is never shown by the stars alone.
