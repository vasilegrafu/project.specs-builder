One piece of feedback in the staff's list.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| unhappy | rated 1 or 2 stars |
| words only | the customer gave no rating |
| rating only | the customer wrote nothing |

## Rules

What a customer left out is left out of the card. Unhappy feedback has a red edge as well as its tag.
