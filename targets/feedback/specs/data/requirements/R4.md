---
id: R4
name: Only staff read feedback
priority: must
serves: G1, G2, G3, G4
---

The website shall show feedback only to staff who have signed in.

## Details

Each member of staff has a sign-in of their own. A developer adds and removes them, and the website has no screen for that.

## Accepted when

- R4.1 Given a person who has not signed in, when they open the address of the staff's list, then they are asked to sign in and see no feedback.
- R4.2 Given a wrong password, when someone tries to sign in, then they are refused and see no feedback.
- R4.3 Given a member of staff with the right password, when they sign in, then they see the list of feedback.
- R4.4 Given a member of staff a developer has removed, when they try to sign in, then they are refused and see no feedback.
- R4.5 Given repeated wrong passwords, when the limit on failed sign-ins is reached, then further attempts are refused for a time, even with the right password.
