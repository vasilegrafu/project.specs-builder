---
id: R5
name: An email address for a reply
priority: should
serves: G2
---

Where a person gives an email address, the website shall keep it with their feedback.

## Accepted when

- R5.1 Given feedback sent with ana@example.com, when staff read it, then they see that address with it.
- R5.2 Given feedback sent with no address, when it arrives, then it is kept and staff see no address with it.
- R5.3 Given an address written as ana-at-example, when the person sends it, then the website says the address does not look right and their words stay on the page.
