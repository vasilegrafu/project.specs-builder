---
id: R8
name: Feedback per month
priority: should
serves: G1, G2, G3, G4
depends on: R2
---

The website shall show staff how many pieces of feedback arrived in each calendar month, leaving out those they removed.

## Accepted when

- R8.1 Given 12 pieces of feedback in March, when staff look at March, then it shows 12.
- R8.2 Given a month with no feedback, when staff look at it, then it shows 0.
