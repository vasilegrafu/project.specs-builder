---
id: R19
name: Delete after 12 months
priority: must
serves: G1, G2, G3, G4
depends on: R2
---

When a piece of feedback is 12 months old, the website shall delete it and the email address kept with it.

## Accepted when

- R19.1 Given feedback that arrived on 3 March 2026, when staff look on 3 March 2027, then it and its email address are gone.
- R19.2 Given feedback that arrived on 4 March 2026, when staff look on 3 March 2027, then it is still there.
- R19.3 Given a customer who opted out on 3 March 2026, when their order is delivered on 3 May 2027, then no request is sent.
