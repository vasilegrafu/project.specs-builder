---
id: R22
name: A record of opt-outs and permissions
priority: should
serves: G4, G5
depends on: R9, R17
---

The website shall keep the date of every opt-out and of every permission to publish.

## Accepted when

- R22.1 Given a customer who opted out on 3 March, when staff look at the record, then it shows that opt-out and 3 March.
- R22.2 Given feedback sent on 3 March with permission to publish, when staff read it, then it shows that permission and 3 March.
- R22.3 Given an opt-out from 3 March 2026, when its customer's feedback has been deleted after 12 months, then the opt-out and its date are still there.
