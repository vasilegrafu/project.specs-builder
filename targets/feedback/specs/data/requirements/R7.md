---
id: R7
name: Satisfaction by month
priority: should
serves: G3
depends on: R6
---

The website shall show staff the average rating of each calendar month.

## Accepted when

- R7.1 Given two ratings in March, one of 2 and one of 4, when staff look at March, then its average shows as 3.
- R7.2 Given a month with no ratings, when staff look at it, then it shows no average, rather than zero.
