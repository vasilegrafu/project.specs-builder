---
id: R14
name: At most one request a month
priority: must
serves: G1, G2, G3, G4, G5
depends on: R13
---

While a customer has received a request in the past month, the website shall send them no other request.

## Accepted when

- R14.1 Given a request sent on 3 March, when another of the customer's orders is delivered on 20 March, then no request is sent.
- R14.2 Given a request sent on 3 March, when another of the customer's orders is delivered on 3 April, then one request is sent.
- R14.3 Given two of a customer's orders delivered on the same day, when both are known, then one request is sent.
- R14.4 Given orders from one email address on the online shop and on a marketplace, when both are delivered within a month, then one request is sent.
