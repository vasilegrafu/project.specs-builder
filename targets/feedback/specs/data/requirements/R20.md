---
id: R20
name: Requests and opt-outs per month
priority: should
serves: G5
depends on: R13, R17
---

The website shall show staff how many requests were sent and how many customers opted out in each calendar month.

## Accepted when

- R20.1 Given 500 requests and 4 opt-outs in March, when staff look at March, then it shows 500 and 4.
- R20.2 Given a month with no requests, when staff look at it, then it shows 0 and 0.
