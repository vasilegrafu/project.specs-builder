---
id: R12
name: Remove feedback
priority: should
serves: G1, G2, G3, G4
depends on: R3
---

When a member of staff removes a piece of feedback, the website shall take it out of the list and the monthly figures.

## Accepted when

- R12.1 Given 12 pieces of feedback in March, when staff remove one, then the list no longer shows it and March shows 11.
- R12.2 Given a removed piece of feedback rated 1 star, when staff look at its month, then its average leaves that rating out.
- R12.3 Given a person who has not signed in, when they try to remove feedback, then nothing is removed.
- R12.4 Given staff press Remove, when the website asks them to confirm and they say no, then the feedback stays.
- R12.5 Given two members of staff remove the same feedback at the same moment, when both are done, then it is gone and neither sees a fault.
