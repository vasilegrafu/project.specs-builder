---
id: R23
name: Sign out
priority: must
serves: G1, G2, G3, G4
depends on: R4
---

When a member of staff signs out, the website shall show no feedback until someone signs in again.

## Accepted when

- R23.1 Given a member of staff who presses Sign out, when it is done, then they see the sign-in page.
- R23.2 Given a member of staff who has signed out, when they press the browser's Back, then they are asked to sign in and see no feedback.
