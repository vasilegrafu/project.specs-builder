---
id: R16
name: A link and QR code to print
priority: should
serves: G1, G2, G3, G4
depends on: R4
---

The website shall give signed-in staff a link and a QR code to the feedback page, to print on receipts and in parcels.

## Accepted when

- R16.1 Given a member of staff who has signed in, when they open the printing page, then they can save the link and the QR code.
- R16.2 Given a QR code on a printed receipt, when a customer scans it with a phone, then the feedback page opens.
- R16.3 Given feedback sent from a printed link, when staff read it, then it reads and counts as any other feedback.
