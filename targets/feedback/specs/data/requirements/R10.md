---
id: R10
name: Unhappy feedback stands out
priority: should
serves: G2
depends on: R3, R6
---

When feedback rated 1 or 2 stars arrives, the website shall mark it in the staff's list.

## Accepted when

- R10.1 Given feedback rated 2 stars, when staff open the list, then it is marked.
- R10.2 Given feedback rated 3 stars, when staff open the list, then it is not marked.
- R10.3 Given feedback with no rating, when staff open the list, then it is not marked.
