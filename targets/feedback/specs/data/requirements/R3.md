---
id: R3
name: Read the feedback
priority: must
serves: G1, G2, G4
depends on: R2
---

The website shall show the business's staff every piece of feedback they have not removed, newest first.

## Accepted when

- R3.1 Given three pieces of feedback, when staff open the list, then all three show, the newest at the top.
- R3.2 Given no feedback yet, when staff open the list, then it says there is no feedback yet.
