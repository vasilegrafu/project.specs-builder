---
id: R17
name: Opting out of requests
priority: must
serves: G5
depends on: R13
---

When a customer opts out, the website shall send them no more requests for feedback.

## Accepted when

- R17.1 Given any request for feedback, when a customer reads it, then it holds a way to opt out.
- R17.2 Given a customer who opted out on 3 March, when their next order is delivered, then no request is sent.
- R17.3 Given a customer who has opted out, when they open the feedback page by a link or QR code, then they can still send feedback.
