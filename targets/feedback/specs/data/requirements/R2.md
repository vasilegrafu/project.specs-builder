---
id: R2
name: Keep every piece of feedback
priority: must
serves: G1, G2, G3, G4
---

When a person sends feedback, the website shall keep it with the date and time it arrived.

## Accepted when

- R2.1 Given feedback sent at 14:05 on 3 March, when staff look at it later, then it shows what was written and 3 March, 14:05.
- R2.2 Given feedback sent yesterday, when the website has been restarted, then the feedback is still there.
