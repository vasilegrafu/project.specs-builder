---
id: R1
name: Give feedback without an account
priority: must
serves: G1, G2, G3, G4
---

The website shall let anyone who opens it send feedback without signing in.

## Accepted when

- R1.1 Given a person with no account, when they open the website, write 'The delivery was late' and send it, then the website says their feedback was received.
- R1.2 Given a person who leaves everything empty, when they send it, then the website says there is nothing to send and keeps nothing.
- R1.3 Given the website cannot keep the feedback, when a person sends it, then the website says it was not received and their words stay on the page to send again.
- R1.4 Given a person who presses Send twice on the same feedback, when both arrive, then one piece of feedback is kept.
