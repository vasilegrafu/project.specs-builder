---
id: R18
name: When delivery cannot be known
priority: must
serves: G1, G2, G3, G4
depends on: R13
---

If the delivery of an order cannot be known, then the website shall send the request 7 days after the order was placed.

## Accepted when

- R18.1 Given an order placed on 3 March with no delivery known, when 10 March arrives, then the customer receives one request.
- R18.2 Given an order placed on 3 March whose delivery becomes known on 6 March, when 10 March arrives, then the customer receives no second request.
- R18.3 Given a customer who received a request on 1 March, when 10 March arrives for an order with no delivery known, then no request is sent.
