---
id: R9
name: Permission to publish
priority: should
serves: G4
---

The website shall let a person say that the business may publish what they wrote.

## Accepted when

- R9.1 Given a person who ticks the permission and sends their feedback, when staff read it, then it shows that it may be published.
- R9.2 Given a person who leaves the permission as it stands when the page opens, when they send their feedback, then the permission is unticked and staff see it may not be published.
