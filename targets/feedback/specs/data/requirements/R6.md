---
id: R6
name: A rating with the feedback
priority: should
serves: G3
---

The website shall let a person give a rating of 1 to 5 stars with their feedback.

## Accepted when

- R6.1 Given a person who picks a rating and sends it, when staff read that feedback, then they see the rating with it.
- R6.2 Given a person who writes a message and picks no rating, when they send it, then it is kept with no rating.
- R6.3 Given a person who picks a rating and writes nothing, when they send it, then it is kept with no message.
- R6.4 Given a person giving feedback, when they pick a rating, then they can pick 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 stars and nothing else.
