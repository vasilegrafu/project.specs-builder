---
id: R11
name: Email staff about an unhappy user
priority: should
serves: G2
depends on: R6
---

When feedback rated 1 or 2 stars arrives, the website shall email every member of staff.

## Accepted when

- R11.1 Given feedback rated 2 stars, when it arrives, then every member of staff receives one email holding the rating and what was written.
- R11.2 Given feedback rated 3 stars, when it arrives, then no email is sent.
- R11.3 Given feedback with no rating, when it arrives, then no email is sent.
- R11.4 Given the email cannot be sent, when feedback rated 1 star arrives, then the feedback is still kept and marked in the staff's list.
