name: Colours and what they mean
---

| Colour | Value | Means | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| swatch accent | #0b6b6b, NovaLife teal | what can be pressed or followed: act here | primary buttons, links, the chosen free time, prices |
| swatch primary-soft | #e3f2f0, pale teal | a quiet ground for what belongs to the action | the home page's opening, a chosen card, a slot under the pointer |
| swatch warm | #f08a5d, coral | warmth and optimism; decoration only | the mark before the name, the rule under the opening, the top of a service's card, the rule over the footer |
| swatch ink | #102a35, deep ink | text, and the staff area | text, the footer, the staff header |
| swatch muted | #4b6470 | what is secondary | labels of facts, lengths, table heads |
| swatch line | #d3dfdd | a border | cards, fields, tables |
| swatch bg | #f6f9f8 | the page | behind everything |
| swatch surface | #ffffff | a surface | the header, cards, fields, dialogs |
| swatch good | #1c7a4a | done | a message that something was booked, saved or cancelled |
| swatch bad | #b42318 | refused or failed | a message that something was refused or failed, a field in error |
| swatch warn | #8a5300 on #fff3dc | take care | the notice that booking is not for emergencies |

Coral is never text and never the only sign of anything: on white it is 2.6 to 1.
