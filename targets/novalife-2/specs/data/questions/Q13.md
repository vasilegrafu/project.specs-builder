Which rules on prices apply to patients who pay themselves, and what must the website show to meet them (R6, R7)? Background read on 2026-10-20, for the adviser to confirm, not settled: under the federal No Surprises Act a provider tells a patient who pays themselves that a good faith estimate of the cost is available -- in a notice on its website, in the office and when a visit is scheduled -- and gives one when a visit is booked some days ahead or when asked; CMS publishes a model notice.

## Why

R7 shows a price per appointment type, and these rules may ask for a notice on the website and an estimate with each booking, which would change booking (R6) and the confirmation
