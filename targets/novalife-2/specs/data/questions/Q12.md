Which rules on health advertising apply to what the website says about the services and about longevity (R2, R4)? Background read on 2026-10-20, for the adviser to confirm, not settled: New York's Education Law 6530(27) and the Regents' rule 8 NYCRR 29.1 forbid a physician's advertising that is false or misleading, guarantees a result, or claims what cannot be substantiated, allow a patient's testimonial only with their written consent and the rule's conditions, and ask that a copy of each advertisement be kept for a year after it last appears; the FTC asks that a health claim rest on competent and reliable scientific evidence.

## Why

a claim the rules do not allow puts the clinic at risk, the longevity wording is where one is likeliest, and the staff change the words themselves (R13), so the rules may also decide who approves a change and what is kept of it
