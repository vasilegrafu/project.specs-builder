---
id: R15
name: Cancel a booking
priority: must
serves: G1
---

When a patient cancels their booking at least 24 hours before it starts, the website shall cancel it and offer its time in booking again.

## Accepted when

- R15.1 Given a booking on November 20 at 10:00 AM, when its patient cancels it on November 19 at 9:00 AM, then it shows as cancelled, and 10:00 AM on November 20 is free again in booking.
- R15.2 Given a booking on November 20 at 10:00 AM, when its patient tries to cancel it on November 19 at 11:00 AM, then the website refuses, says that a booking less than 24 hours away is cancelled by phoning the clinic, and gives the clinic's phone number.
- R15.3 Given a person who phones to cancel their booking, when the staff member who answers cancels it on the website, then it is cancelled and its time is free again, however close to its start.
- R15.4 Given a patient who wants another time, when they look at their booking, then the website offers to cancel it and book again; it does not move a booking.
- R15.5 Given a patient signed in, when they look for another patient's booking, then they cannot see it or cancel it (R17).
