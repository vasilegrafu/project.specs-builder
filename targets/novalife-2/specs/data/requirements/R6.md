---
id: R6
name: Book an appointment online
priority: must
serves: G1, G2
---

When a signed-in patient chooses an appointment type and a doctor's free time, the website shall book that time and confirm the booking on the next screen.

## Why

The clinic chose that visitors book a free time and are confirmed on the spot, rather than send a request it answers the same working day (D1). The goals count what people book themselves: 80% of a month's bookings, and 100 new patients a month, 12 months after launch (G1, G2).

## Accepted when

- R6.1 Given a doctor free on 20 November at 10:00, when a signed-in patient chooses an appointment type that doctor gives, that doctor and that time, and books, then the next screen confirms the booking with the appointment type, the doctor, the day and the time, and 10:00 that day is no longer free.
- R6.2 Given a person who phones the clinic, when the staff member who answers books on the website with that person's first and last name, date of birth and phone, and their email where they have one, then the booking is made in that person's name, as if they had booked it themselves, and is recorded as made by the staff for them.
- R6.3 Given a caller with no email, when the staff book for them with a phone alone, then the booking is made without an account, and the caller cancels it by phoning (R15).
- R6.4 Given a caller with no phone, when the staff try to book for them, then the website refuses and says that a phone number is always needed.
- R6.5 Given the staff booking for a caller, when the form opens, then it marks the phone as required and the email as wanted but not required.
- R6.6 Given a visitor who is not signed in, when they choose a time and book, then the website asks them to sign in or create an account (R16), keeps the time chosen, and books it once they have.
- R6.7 Given an appointment type, when it is chosen in booking, then booking shows only the doctors who give it and only their free times.
- R6.8 Given any appointment type, when a visitor books it, then nothing is asked of them first: no earlier consultation is needed for any type.
- R6.9 Given a day on which no doctor giving the chosen appointment type is free, when the visitor chooses it, then the website says so and shows the nearest days with a free time.
- R6.10 Given a time another visitor booked while this one was booking, when this one books it, then the website says the time was just taken, books nothing, and shows the nearest free times.
- R6.11 Given a booking that fails to be made, when the visitor books, then the website says so, books nothing, keeps what was written, and offers to try again.
- R6.12 Given booking, when it opens, then it asks for no medical details, and says that symptoms and results are discussed with the doctor, not written in the form.
