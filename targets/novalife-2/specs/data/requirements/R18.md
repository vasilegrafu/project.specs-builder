---
id: R18
name: Staff roles
priority: must
serves: G1
---

The website shall let each member of staff do only what their role allows: an administrator everything in the staff area; the front desk booking and cancelling for callers and setting any doctor's free times; a doctor setting their own free times and days away.

## Accepted when

- R18.1 Given a signed-in administrator, when they open the staff area, then they can change the pages, the services and their appointment types, the prices, the team, the opening hours, every doctor's free times and the staff accounts.
- R18.2 Given a signed-in member of the front desk, when they book or cancel for a caller, or set a doctor's free times, then the website does it.
- R18.3 Given a signed-in member of the front desk, when they try to change a page, a price, an appointment type or a staff account, then the website refuses and says that only an administrator can.
- R18.4 Given a signed-in doctor, when they set their own free times or mark themselves away, then the website does it (R14).
- R18.5 Given a signed-in doctor, when they try to change another doctor's free times, a page, a price or a booking, then the website refuses.
