---
id: R3
name: How NovaLife is different
priority: must
serves: G2
---

The website shall show how NovaLife differs from the traditional approach, point by point: proactive and preventive rather than reactive; a connected view of health rather than separate appointments and results; personalized, evidence-based plans rather than general recommendations; long-term wellbeing rather than the current illness alone; a digital-first experience rather than paper-based or fragmented processes.

## Accepted when

- R3.1 Given the page on what makes NovaLife different, when it opens, then it shows the five comparisons, each as the traditional approach beside NovaLife's.
- R3.2 Given the same page, when it opens, then it says "Technology supports medical professionals; it does not replace their judgment."
