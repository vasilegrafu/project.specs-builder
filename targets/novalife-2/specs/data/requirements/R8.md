The website shall show the clinicians who work at NovaLife, each with their name, role and qualifications.

## Accepted when

- R8.1 Given the Team page, when it opens, then each clinician shows with their name, photograph, role and qualifications.
- R8.2 Given a clinician who has left the clinic, when the Team page opens, then they are not on it.
