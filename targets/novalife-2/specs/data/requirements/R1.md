---
id: R1
name: The home page presents NovaLife
priority: must
serves: G1, G2
---

When anyone opens the website's home page, the website shall show the clinic's name, its tagline, its mission and its central idea, with a way from there to each of the four services and to booking.

## Accepted when

- R1.1 Given the home page, when it opens, then it shows "NovaLife Clinic", "The Future of Health" and the mission "Help people understand their health, act earlier, and stay healthier for longer."
- R1.2 Given the home page, when it opens, then it shows the central idea "Don't just treat illness. Help people protect their health today and preserve it for the future."
- R1.3 Given the home page, when one of the four services is chosen on it, then that service's page opens (R2).
- R1.4 Given the home page, when booking is chosen on it, then booking opens (R6).
