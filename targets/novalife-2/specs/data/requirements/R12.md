---
id: R12
name: Staff sign in
priority: must
serves: G1
---

When a member of staff signs in with their email and password, the website shall open the staff area to them.

## Accepted when

- R12.1 Given a member of staff with an account, when they sign in with their email and password, then the staff area opens.
- R12.2 Given a wrong password, or an email with no account, when someone signs in, then sign-in is refused with one message that does not say which of the two was wrong.
- R12.3 Given someone not signed in, when they open an address of the staff area, then sign-in opens in its place.
- R12.4 Given a signed-in member of staff, when they sign out, then the staff area closes, and its addresses ask for sign-in again.
- R12.5 Given a member of staff who has forgotten their password, when they ask to reset it on staff sign-in, then the website sends a link to their email that lets them set a new one, and says the same whether or not the email has a staff account.
