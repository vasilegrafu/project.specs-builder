---
id: R13
name: Staff change what the website says
priority: must
serves: G1
---

The website shall let a signed-in administrator change the text and pictures of every page, the services and their appointment types, the opening hours, the prices and the team, and show each change to visitors once it is saved.

## Accepted when

- R13.1 Given a signed-in administrator, when they change a service's description and save, then the service's page shows the new description.
- R13.2 Given a signed-in administrator, when they change an appointment type's price and save, then its service's page and booking show the new price (R7).
- R13.3 Given a signed-in administrator, when they add an appointment type with its service, its length, its price and the doctors who give it, and save, then booking offers it with those doctors (R6).
- R13.4 Given a signed-in administrator, when they add a clinician with a name, a photograph, a role and qualifications and save, then the Team page shows them (R8).
- R13.5 Given a signed-in administrator, when they change the opening hours and save, then the Contact page shows the new hours (R5).
- R13.6 Given a change that is not saved, when the administrator leaves the page, then the website warns that the change will be lost.
- R13.7 Given a change that fails to be saved, when the administrator saves, then the website says so, keeps the change written, and visitors still see what was there before.
