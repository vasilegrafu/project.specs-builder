---
id: R10
name: Privacy
priority: must
serves: G2
---

The website shall show, on a page reached from every page and from booking, what it keeps about a visitor, why, who reads it and for how long.

## Accepted when

- R10.1 Given any page, when Privacy is chosen, then the privacy notice opens.
- R10.2 Given booking, when it opens, then it leads to the privacy notice before anything is booked.
- R10.3 Given the privacy notice, when it opens, then it says what a booking keeps, why, who reads it and for how long.
