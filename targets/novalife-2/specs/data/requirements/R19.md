---
id: R19
name: Staff accounts
priority: must
serves: G1
---

When an administrator adds a member of staff with their name, email and role, the website shall create their account and send them a link to set their password.

## Accepted when

- R19.1 Given a signed-in administrator, when they add Dr. Lee with her email and the role doctor, then Dr. Lee receives a link by email, sets her password with it, and signs in as a doctor (R12).
- R19.2 Given an email that already has a staff account, when an administrator adds it again, then the website refuses and says so.
- R19.3 Given a member of staff an administrator has removed, when they try to sign in, then sign-in is refused.
- R19.4 Given one administrator left, when an administrator tries to remove that account or change its role, then the website refuses, so that the staff area always has an administrator.
- R19.5 Given the website on the day it launches, when it is first opened, then one administrator account exists, set up at launch, and no other.
