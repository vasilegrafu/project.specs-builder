## Rules

- Each role sees only the pages it may open: an administrator all, the front desk Bookings, Book for a caller and Free times, a doctor Free times alone. (R18)
- Deep ink, where the public header is white, so that the staff always know they are in the staff area.
- Its look is the proposal of the foundations, 2026-10-20, to be changed with them. (foundations)
