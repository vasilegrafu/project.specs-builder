One appointment type a visitor can book: its name, its length, its price, and the way to book it. Used on a service's page and in booking, so that the price is seen before anything is booked (R7).

## Rules

- The price is always shown, in US dollars with no cents where there are none: $120. (R7)
- A type with no doctor who gives it is not shown. (R6)
- Cards of a service sit in a row, each at least 220 pixels wide, and wrap.
- Its look is the proposal of the foundations, 2026-10-20, to be changed with them. (foundations)
