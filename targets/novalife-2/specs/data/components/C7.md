name: Field
---

Where a person writes or chooses something: a name, a date, an appointment type. Its label always stands above it, and what is wrong with it is said beneath it.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| default | waiting to be filled |
| with the focus | being written in: a teal ring |
| in error | refused: a red edge, and the reason beneath it, C8 |
| disabled | fixed, as a doctor's own name on SC16 |

## Rules

- The label above the field, semibold, in the product's words; never a placeholder in its place.
- 44 pixels high, and no wider than 420 pixels, or 720 for a text that runs long.
- What is required is said in the label, (required), and what is optional, (if they have one).
- Its look is the proposal of the foundations, 2026-10-20, to be changed with them. (foundations)
