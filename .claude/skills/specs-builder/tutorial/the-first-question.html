The owner's real answer

We take about 20 cake orders a week by phone, usually while we're serving at the counter, and that's when mistakes happen. I want at least 15 of those 20 to come through the website within six months of it going live. People also ring just to ask when we're open and where we are. I've never counted those, so write not measured. I'd like those calls cut by half in the same six months. Showing what we bake is for the first goal: people choose their cake before they order.

What Claude does

It writes two goals, and every requirement after this has to serve one of them. If you tried to add a requirement with no goal, it would be refused.

Why it matters

A website can do everything you listed and still not help the business. The goal is how you'll know, and it's the reason each feature exists.
