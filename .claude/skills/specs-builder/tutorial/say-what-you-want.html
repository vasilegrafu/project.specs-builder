Use specs-builder. I run a small bakery called Mill Lane Bakery and I want a small website. People should be able to see what we bake, order a cake for pick-up, and find our opening hours and address. My staff should be able to see the orders and mark them ready. Keep it as small as you can: about 10 requirements, 5 screens and 4 flows, with a design system, the components the screens share, and a working prototype of every screen.

What Claude does

It reads the skill's instructions and sets up a specification in the folder, with the application that shows it. Then it stops, because there's something it can't write without you.

Why it matters

You don't write a specification. You say what you want in your own words, and react to what comes back.

If yours looks different

Use your own idea if you like. The steps work the same way, though the details and numbers will differ.
