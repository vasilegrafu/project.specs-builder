What Claude asks

Then come the facts only you know. Claude offers options where it can, and recommends the smallest. These are the real questions and answers:

How to answer

IfSayWhat happens
You knowJust say itIt's written in the right place, with where it came from
You're not sure"I don't know yet"It stays an open question, so it won't be forgotten
You'd like advice"What would you do?"You get a recommendation, and you still say yes
It's too much"Leave that out"It's removed, not hidden
You're out of time"Later"It waits on the open list

What Claude does

Each answer goes where it belongs, and a choice becomes a decision with its reason. Two days' notice became a rule, and the form follows it. When the last question is answered, it finishes the design system and draws every screen.

Why it matters

Claude never guesses a number or a law. If you don't know something, the plan says so openly, which is far safer than a guess nobody noticed.

If yours looks different

Real sessions take a while, because Claude reads, writes and checks its own work. The bakery took over an hour of working time, including one false start. You can leave it running.
