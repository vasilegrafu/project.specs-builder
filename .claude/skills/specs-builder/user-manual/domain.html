Read it like this

  1. Read the terms first. Half of all arguments are about words.
  2. Read the rules. They're the shortest statement of how the business works.
  3. For a rule, read Linked from. It's what depends on the rule.
  4. For an entity, read Lifecycle: the states it moves through and what moves it.
The limit itself

Here it is: B1, Up to five of a product. A cart holds at most five of one product, whether added at once or over several times. The entity N2 repeats the limit in its Fields, its quantity of one to five. Repeating it is allowed, but N2 goes stale when B1 changes, so nobody can forget it.

Mistakes people make

  • Writing a rule inside a requirement. Then two requirements can disagree. Write it once, and point at it.
  • Thinking like a database. An entity is what the business keeps. How it's stored is for the people who build it.
