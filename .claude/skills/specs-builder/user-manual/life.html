Part 1: From zero

A specification's life

A specification isn't written once and filed. It moves through a few stages, and comes back round whenever the product changes.

The stages

StageWhat's happeningHow it moves on
An ideaYou have a goal, or a product that already existsClaude writes it up, asks you, or reads what exists
DraftFacts are added, questions raised, additions confirmedNothing stands in the way, and you accept it
AgreedIt's accepted, and refuses every changeWhoever builds it starts
Built fromThe product is made from it, one piece at a timeThe product moves on, and it goes back to draft

Where your shop is

Voltline is agreed. It describes the shop as it runs today, and the team can start. One question is still open, Q1: how long is a visitor's cart kept? But that one's for the engineers, and only questions for the product side hold up an agreement.

That's also why changing it begins by reopening it.
