---
id: R10
name: The night's log, step by step
priority: should
serves: G2
from: S4, the log of 10 October; S2, stock_sync.py, the log
---

The stock sync shall write a line to the day's log for each step of a run: its start, each try of the feed that failed, the feed fetched with its rows and the time it was made, each SKU listed again, the SKUs not sold, the SKUs that differ, those written, the report and the mail, and how the run ended.

## Accepted when

- R10.1 Given the night of 10 October, when the run ends, then the log holds, in order: "starting: feed" and its address, "feed answered 503 on try 1", "fetched 1,214 rows, as of 2026-10-10 00:58 UTC", "line 388: AC-VLT-65 listed again; the last row wins", "41 SKU(s) of the feed are not sold by the shop", "23 SKU(s) differ from the shop's stock", "written: 23 SKU(s)", "report written" with its place, the low and the out and the mail, and "done: 23 SKU(s) changed".
- R10.2 Given a run refused or stopped, when it ends, then its last line says why, as the mail does.
- R10.3 Given two runs on one day, when both end, then both are in that day's log, one after the other.
