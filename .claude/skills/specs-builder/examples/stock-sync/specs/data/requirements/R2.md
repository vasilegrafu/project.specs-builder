---
id: R2
name: Fetch the feed, three times at most
priority: must
serves: G1, G2
enforces: B3
from: S2, stock_sync.py, the fetch; S4, the log of 10 October
---

When a run starts, the stock sync shall fetch the supplier's feed, and where the supplier does not answer with it, try again up to two more times, ten minutes apart.

## Accepted when

- R2.1 Given the supplier answers 503 at 02:31 and the feed at 02:41, when the run fetches it, then the log shows the 503 on the first try and the feed fetched on the second, and the run goes on.
- R2.2 Given the supplier answers nothing within 30 seconds, when the run fetches, then that try counts as failed and the next comes ten minutes later.
- R2.3 Given three tries fail, when the third has failed, then the run is refused with "the feed could not be fetched in 3 tries", the shop's stock is as it was, and the morning is mailed, R6.
