When a run ends with any SKU low, out of stock or no longer in the feed, or without doing its work for any reason, the stock sync shall mail the operations team the night's report or the reason it stopped.

## Accepted when

- R6.1 Given a run that wrote 23 SKUs, 4 of them now at 3 or fewer and 2 at none, when it ends, then the report is kept for the day and mailed with the subject "Stock sync 2026-10-10: low stock": the time in UTC and the outcome, "23 SKU(s) changed", the 4 low and the 2 out by SKU, and each change as "SKU: before → after".
- R6.2 Given a night with nothing to do and one SKU at 2, when the run ends, then the mail comes all the same, with that SKU among the low.
- R6.3 Given a night with nothing to do, no SKU at 3 or fewer and every SKU of the shop in the feed, when the run ends, then no mail comes, and the log says nothing changed.
- R6.4 Given the feed refused or a write refused, when the run ends, then the mail's subject says FAILED and why, and its body says the reason and to see the runbook.
- R6.5 Given the catalogue does not answer with the shop's stock, or any other stop the runbook does not name, when the run ends, then the mail's subject says FAILED, and the run ends with exit 5.
- R6.6 Given a run held off by another's lock, when it ends, then the mail says so, R7.
