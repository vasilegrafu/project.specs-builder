What Place order calls: the cart becomes an order, placed, with the address and the way to pay.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U2 or U3, signed in | place an order from their own cart | 401 signed out |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| address | body | an address: name, street, city, zip, each required | yes | the delivery address, N3, R6 |
| payment | body | string: card, paypal, invoice | yes | one of the three ways to pay, R6 |
| code | body | string | no | a discount code, R5 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 201 | the cart holds lines and each is in stock | the order, placed: its number, date, status, lines, total and address |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | not signed in | {"error": "not signed in"} |
| 409 | the cart is empty, or a line is out of stock | {"error": "the cart is empty, or a line is out of stock"} , which the checkout shows above Place order |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | no: each call that passes makes an order; the cart is emptied, so a second call is refused as empty |
| Version | v1, in the path |
