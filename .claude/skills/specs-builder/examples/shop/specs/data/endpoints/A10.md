What Sign out in the account menu calls: the session ends, the cart is kept.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U2 or U3, signed in | end their own session | signed out already: 204 all the same |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| session | header | the session cookie | yes | the caller's own session, N4 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 204 | always | nothing: the cookie is cleared, and the cart is kept for the next sign-in |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 204 | no session to end | nothing: the call does no harm twice |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes |
| Version | v1, in the path |
