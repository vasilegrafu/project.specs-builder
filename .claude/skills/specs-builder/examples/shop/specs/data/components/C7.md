Where a user types: its label above, the field, and when it is refused, why, under it.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| default | always |
| refused | after the form was refused: marked red, what was typed kept, the reason under it |

## Rules

- The label above the field, in bold; never a placeholder in its place.
- A refused field keeps what was typed and says why under itself, in one short sentence.
