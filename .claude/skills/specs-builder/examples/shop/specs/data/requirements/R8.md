When a visitor sends their name, email, a password typed twice and the terms accepted, the shop shall make their account and sign them in, and refuse a form with a field wrong by saying how many need a look above it and why under each.

## Accepted when

- R8.1 Given a name, an email no account has, a password of eight characters with a number typed twice and the terms ticked, when Create account is selected, then the account is made and the customer is signed in.
- R8.2 Given "ana@" as the email, a password of five letters and a repeat that differs, when Create account is selected, then the page says "Three things need a look." above the form, and under the fields "That is not an email address.", "At least 8 characters, with a number." and "The two passwords differ."
- R8.3 Given the terms not ticked, when Create account is selected, then the form is refused and nothing is made.
