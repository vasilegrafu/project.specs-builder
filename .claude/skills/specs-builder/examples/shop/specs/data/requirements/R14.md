---
id: R14
name: Only the staff reach the admin
priority: must
serves: G2
enforces: B4
from: S2, the admin's door, V31; S3, PATCH /orders/{number}, 403
---

When someone who is not the admin opens a page or a call of the admin's part, the shop shall refuse it: a page that says the part is for the staff, with a way back to the shop, and 403 for a call.

## Accepted when

- R14.1 Given a customer who is not the admin, when /admin/orders is opened, then the page says "This part of the shop is for its staff. You are signed in as a customer." and offers Back to the shop.
- R14.2 Given a visitor, when /admin/orders is opened, then sign in comes first.
- R14.3 Given a customer's session, when an order is moved by the call, then the answer is 403 and the order is unchanged.
