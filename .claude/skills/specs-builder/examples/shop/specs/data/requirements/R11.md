---
id: R11
name: Keep the profile
priority: must
serves: G2
from: S2, the profile, V23; S3, GET and PATCH /account
---

When a customer opens their profile, the shop shall show their name, email, phone and delivery address to change, a way to change the password, and a way to delete the account.

## Accepted when

- R11.1 Given a signed-in customer, when Profile is opened, then the fields hold what the account has, and Save changes writes what was changed.
- R11.2 Given the current password and a new one, when Change the password is selected, then the new one signs the customer in from then on.
- R11.3 Given a field refused, when Save changes is selected, then the field says why and nothing is changed.
