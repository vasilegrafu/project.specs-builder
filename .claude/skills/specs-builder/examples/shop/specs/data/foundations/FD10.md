name: Accessibility
---

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Contrast | --color-ink on --color-surface for text, --color-surface on --color-brand for C1: both above 4.5:1 |
| Focus | a 3px --color-brand ring, 2px off the element, on every link, button and field (E46) |
| Keyboard | Tab reaches every control in reading order, the header first; Skip to content comes first of all |
| A screen reader | the stars are read as "4.6 out of 5"; C8 is an alert, announced when it appears; a refused field is described by its reason |
| Colour | never alone: a stock, a status and a refusal say their words |
