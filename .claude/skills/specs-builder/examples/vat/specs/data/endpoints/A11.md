What a person at the shell runs for the net in a gross price.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U2, U3: anyone at a shell where vatkit is installed | ask the net in any gross price | nothing is refused for who runs it: vatkit has no keys and no accounts |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| amount | argument | an amount: 100, 119.00 | yes | never below zero, B7, R8 |
| country | argument | a member state's two letters in either case | yes | a member state, EL for GR, B5, R1 |
| --category | option | books, food or medicine | no | a category with a reduced rate in that member state, B4, R1 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| prints and ends with 0 | the amount, the country and the category are taken | the net of A4, written as the country writes it: 111,21 € for 119 of books in DE |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| prints to the error stream and ends with 2 | the amount, the country or the category is refused | "vatkit: " and the refusal: "vatkit: CH is not a member state of the EU" |

## Example

```text
$ vatkit net 119 DE --category books
111,21 €
```
