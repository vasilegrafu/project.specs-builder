What a program calls for the rate of a sale in a member state: the standard rate, or the reduced rate of a category.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1, U3: any program that imports vatkit | ask the rate of any member state, and of any category it has a reduced rate for | nothing is refused for who calls: vatkit has no keys and no accounts |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| country | argument | string: a member state's two letters in either case | yes | a member state, EL for GR, B5, R1 |
| category | argument | string: books, food or medicine; or None | no | a category with a reduced rate in that member state; none for the standard rate, B4, R1 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| returns | the member state is one of the 27 and, where a category is given, it has a reduced rate for it | the rate, a Decimal fraction: 0.19 for DE, 0.07 for books in DE |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| raises UnknownCountry | the country is not a member state, or none | "CH is not a member state of the EU" |
| raises UnknownCategory | the member state has no reduced rate for the category | "DK has no reduced rate for books" |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Version | the rates are those in force on the day the release names, vatkit.rates.AS_OF: 1 January 2026 in 2.3.0, B8 |
| Idempotent | yes: the same call answers the same within one release |

## Example

```python
from vatkit import rate_for

rate_for("DE")              # Decimal('0.19')
rate_for("DE", "books")     # Decimal('0.07')
rate_for("EL")              # Decimal('0.24'), Greece
```
