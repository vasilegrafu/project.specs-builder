What a person at the shell runs for the rate of a member state, or of a category in it.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U2, U3: anyone at a shell where vatkit is installed | ask the rate of any member state | nothing is refused for who runs it: vatkit has no keys and no accounts |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| country | argument | a member state's two letters in either case | yes | a member state, EL for GR, B5, R1 |
| --category | option | books, food or medicine | no | a category with a reduced rate in that member state, B4, R1 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| prints and ends with 0 | the member state and the category are taken | the rate of A1 as a percent: 19%, 25.5% |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| prints to the error stream and ends with 2 | the country or the category is refused | "vatkit: DK has no reduced rate for books" |

## Example

```text
$ vatkit rate DE --category books
7%
```
