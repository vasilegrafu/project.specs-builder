---
id: R2
name: Give the VAT on a net price
priority: must
serves: G1
enforces: B2
from: S1, vat_of in compute.py; S2, the tests of the VAT
---

When a program asks for the VAT on a net price in a member state, vatkit shall give the net times the rate of R1, to the cent, a half cent rounded up.

## Accepted when

- R2.1 Given 100.00 in DE, when the VAT is asked, then it is 19.00.
- R2.2 Given 0.05 in DE, whose VAT is 0.0095, when the VAT is asked, then it is 0.01.
- R2.3 Given 0.02 in DE, whose VAT is 0.0038, when the VAT is asked, then it is 0.00.
- R2.4 Given a country, a category or an amount that R1 or R8 refuses, when the VAT is asked, then it is refused in the same words, and no VAT is given.
