When a program asks for the rate of a sale in a member state, with a category or none, vatkit shall give that state's standard rate, or the reduced rate of the category.

## Accepted when

- R1.1 Given DE and no category, when the rate is asked, then it is 0.19.
- R1.2 Given DE and books, when the rate is asked, then it is 0.07.
- R1.3 Given fr in small letters, when the rate is asked, then France's 0.20 is given.
- R1.4 Given EL, and given GR, when the rate is asked, then both give Greece's 0.24.
- R1.5 Given DK and books, Denmark having no reduced rate, when the rate is asked, then it is refused with "DK has no reduced rate for books", and no rate is given.
- R1.6 Given CH, when the rate is asked, then it is refused with "CH is not a member state of the EU".
