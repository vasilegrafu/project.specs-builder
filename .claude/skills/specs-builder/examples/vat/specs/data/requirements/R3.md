---
id: R3
name: Give the gross from a net price
priority: must
serves: G1
enforces: B2
from: S1, gross in compute.py; S2, the test of the gross
---

When a program asks for the gross of a net price in a member state, vatkit shall give the net to the cent plus the VAT on it of R2.

## Accepted when

- R3.1 Given 100 in FR, when the gross is asked, then it is 120.00.
- R3.2 Given 10.00 of books in DE, when the gross is asked, then it is 10.70.
- R3.3 Given a country, a category or an amount that R1 or R8 refuses, when the gross is asked, then it is refused in the same words.
