---
id: R8
name: Refuse what is not an amount
priority: must
serves: G1
enforces: B1, B7
from: S1, amount in money.py and the refusals in errors.py; S2, the tests of a float and of an amount below zero
---

If an amount given to vatkit is a float, is not a number, or is below zero, then vatkit shall refuse it as a bad amount, saying which amount and why, and give no figure.

## Accepted when

- R8.1 Given 0.1 as a float, when its VAT is asked, then it is refused with "0.1: give a Decimal, a whole number or a string, never a float".
- R8.2 Given abc, when its VAT is asked, then it is refused with "'abc' is not an amount".
- R8.3 Given -1, when its VAT is asked, then it is refused with "'-1' is below zero: a refund is a sale of its own, written as a credit".
- R8.4 Given 0, when its VAT is asked, then it is taken, and the VAT is 0.00.
- R8.5 Given a program that catches a ValueError, when vatkit refuses anything, then the program catches it.
