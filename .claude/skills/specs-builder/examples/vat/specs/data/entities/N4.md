id: N4
name: VAT number
---

The number a business is registered under for VAT in its member state: two letters, then the rest in that state's form.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning | From |
|---|---|---|
| two letters | its member state's, EL for Greece, B5 |  |
| the rest | in the member state's form where vatkit has one: nine digits for DE; ten states have a form, the 17 others are checked on their two letters alone |  |
| as filed | the two letters and the rest in capitals, with no spaces, dots or dashes: DE123456789 |  |
