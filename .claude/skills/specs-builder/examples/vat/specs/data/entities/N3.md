The share of a net price a member state takes as VAT on a sale: its standard rate, or the reduced rate of a category.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning | From |
|---|---|---|
| member state | the 27, by their two letters, EL for GR |  |
| category | none for the standard rate; books, food or medicine for a reduced one |  |
| rate | a fraction: 0.19 is 19%, 0.255 is 25.5% |  |
| the day the rates hold | the day on which the release's rates are those in force: 1 January 2026 in 2.3.0 |  |
