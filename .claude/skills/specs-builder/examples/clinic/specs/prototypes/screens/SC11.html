Getting here

Larkfield station is a six-minute walk. Buses 12 and 40 stop at Mill Lane. There is step-free access from the car park, and a lift to the first floor.

Prescriptions

Ask for a repeat prescription at the front desk or by phone; allow two working days.

We keep your name, date of birth, contact details and the record of your visits, as the law on medical records requires, for as long as you are a patient and ten years after.

We send email and text messages only about your visits: a confirmation when one is booked, a reminder the day before, and a note when one is cancelled. We never pass your details to anyone outside the practice without your consent, except where the law requires it.

To see what we hold, or to have it corrected, write to us.
