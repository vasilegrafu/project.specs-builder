---
id: R5
name: See my visits
priority: must
serves: G2, G3
from: S2, my visits with visits and with none, and three visits, V10 to V14
---

When a patient signed in opens My visits, the site shall list the visits coming up and the past ones apart, each with its number, when, with whom, what about and its status, and open each to a page of its own.

## Accepted when

- R5.1 Given a patient with two visits booked and two past, when My visits opens, then Coming up lists LC-2041 and LC-2033, and Past visits lists LC-1987, attended, and LC-1950, cancelled.
- R5.2 Given a patient with no visit booked, when My visits opens, then it says "No visits booked. Your next one shows here once it is booked." and offers Book a visit.
- R5.3 Given a visit that has taken place, when it opens, then it says "This visit has taken place. Nothing more to do with it." and offers nothing more.
- R5.4 Given a cancelled visit, when it opens, then it says the day it was cancelled.
