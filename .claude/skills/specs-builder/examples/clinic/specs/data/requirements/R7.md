When the day before a booked visit comes, the site shall send its patient a text message that reminds them of the visit, to the phone on their account.

## Accepted when

- R7.1 Given LC-2041, booked for 16 October 2026, and a phone on the account, when 15 October 2026 comes, then a text message reminds the patient of the visit.
- R7.2 Given a visit cancelled before the day before, when that day comes, then no reminder is sent for it.
- R7.3 Given an account with no phone, when the day before comes, then no text message is sent.
