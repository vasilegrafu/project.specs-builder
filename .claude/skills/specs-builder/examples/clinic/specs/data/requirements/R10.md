id: R10
name: Keep my contact details
priority: must
serves: G2, G3
enforces: B4
from: S2, my account, V20
---

When a patient signed in saves My account, the site shall keep the email and the phone they give, and keep the name and the date of birth as they are, which only the front desk changes.

## Accepted when

- R10.1 Given a new phone typed in Phone, when Save is selected, then the account keeps it, and the next reminder goes to it.
- R10.2 Given a new email, when Save is selected, then the account keeps it, and the patient signs in with it.
- R10.3 Given the account, when My account opens, then it says "To change your name or date of birth, bring an ID to the front desk.", and a name typed and saved is not kept.
