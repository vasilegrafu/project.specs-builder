When anyone opens Doctors, the site shall list the six of the team, each with their picture, name, role and working days, and open each to a page of its own with what they look after, their usual times and a way to book a visit with them.

## Accepted when

- R1.1 Given the team of six, when Doctors is opened, then the six are listed, four doctors, a nurse and a physiotherapist, each with their role and working days.
- R1.2 Given Dr Amara Okafor, when her name is selected, then her page shows "General practitioner · Monday to Thursday", what she looks after, and her usual times from 08:30 to 16:00.
- R1.3 Given Sam Whitfield, who is not a doctor, when Sam's page opens, then its button reads "Book a visit with Sam", where a doctor's reads with their surname.
