When a patient selects Book a visit on the page of one of the team, the site shall open the booking form with that one already chosen.

## Accepted when

- R4.1 Given Dr Amara Okafor's page, when "Book a visit with Okafor" is selected, then the form opens with Dr Amara Okafor chosen, and the Time offers her usual times.
- R4.2 Given the form opened so, when another of the team is chosen, then the form takes the new choice.
- R4.3 Given Book a visit in the header, when it is selected, then the form opens with nobody chosen, as today.
