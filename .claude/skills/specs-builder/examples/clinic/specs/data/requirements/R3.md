---
id: R3
name: Refuse a booking that cannot be made
priority: must
serves: G1, G3
enforces: B1, B2
from: S2, the booking form refused and a day with no free time, V7 and V8
---

If the booking form is sent with a day that has passed, no time, an email that is not one, or a time that is not free, then the site shall book nothing and say above the form what needs a look, each field saying why under itself, with what was typed kept.

## Accepted when

- R3.1 Given 3 October 2026 as the day on 11 October, no time, and "priya@" as the email, when Book the visit is selected, then the page says "Three things need a look before the visit can be booked.", the Day says "That day has passed. Choose a day from tomorrow on.", the Time "Choose a time." and the email "That is not an email address.", and the doctor and what it is about stay as typed.
- R3.2 Given Dr Tom Hale on 20 October 2026, a day with no free time, when Book the visit is selected, then the page says "Dr Tom Hale has no free time on 20 October 2026. The nearest free times are on 23 October: 10:30 and 15:00.", and the form keeps what was chosen.
- R3.3 Given 11 October 2026, when the Day is opened, then it offers 12 October 2026 to 12 January 2027 and no day outside them.
