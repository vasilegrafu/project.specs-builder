---
id: R8
name: Sign in
priority: must
serves: G3
from: S2, sign in and its refusal, V17 and V18
---

When a patient sends their email and password, the site shall sign them in, keep them signed in on the device where they ask it, and refuse a wrong pair above the form with both fields marked and the email kept.

## Accepted when

- R8.1 Given the patient's email and password, when Sign in is selected, then My visits opens, and the header shows My visits, the patient's first name and Sign out.
- R8.2 Given a wrong email or password, when Sign in is selected, then the page says "The email or the password is wrong." above the form, both fields are marked, and the email stays.
- R8.3 Given a patient who has forgotten the password, when "Forgotten your password?" is selected, then Contact opens, where they write to the clinic.
