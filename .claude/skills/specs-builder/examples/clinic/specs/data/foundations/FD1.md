| Colour | Value | Means | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| swatch green | --color-green | the clinic; it can be pressed | C1, the edge and words of C2, links, the mark |
| swatch green-dark | --color-green-dark | under the pointer | C1 and links under the pointer |
| swatch green-pale | --color-green-pale | booked; a quiet ground | Booked in C7, C2 under the pointer, a row under the pointer |
| swatch bad | --color-bad | refused, or cannot be undone | C3, C9's refusal, a refused field in C8, Cancelled in C7 |
| swatch bad-pale | --color-bad-pale | the ground of a refusal | C9's refusal, Cancelled in C7 |
| swatch notice | --color-notice | take note | C9's notice of a day with no free time |
| swatch ink | --color-ink | the words | every heading and body text |
| swatch muted | --color-muted | said in passing | a role, a hint, a note, the footer, a label of the facts |
| swatch line | --color-line | an edge | borders of cards, tables, fields and the header's foot |
| swatch bg | --color-bg | the page | the page's ground; white, --color-surface, for cards, forms and the header |
| swatch notice-pale | --color-notice-pale | the ground of a note | C9's notice |
| swatch notice-line | --color-notice-line | a note's edge | the border of C9's notice |
| swatch bad-line | --color-bad-line | a refused field's edge | the border of a field C8 refuses |
| swatch focus | --color-focus | the keyboard is here | the focus ring on every link, button and field |
| swatch attended | --color-attended | attended | Attended in C7 |
| swatch attended-soft | --color-attended-soft | the ground of attended | Attended in C7 |
