name: Patterns
---

| Pattern | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A refused form | Says how many things need a look above the form, C9; each field says its own reason under itself, C8; what was typed is kept. | Three things need a look before the visit can be booked. |
| What cannot be had | Says so above the form, C9's notice, with what can be had instead. | The nearest free times are on 23 October: 10:30 and 15:00. |
| What cannot be undone | A C3 opens a page that says what will be lost, with the C3 that does it and a C2 that keeps it. | This cannot be undone. |
| Nothing to show | Says so in a sentence, and offers the way on. | No visits booked. Book a visit |
| Done | A title that says it is done, the facts, where the email went, and the two ways on. | Your visit is booked |
| Dates and times | The day, the month in words, the year; the time on 24 hours. | 16 October 2026 at 09:30 |
