What a form says above itself after it was sent: what is refused, or a notice of what cannot be had and what can.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| refusal | a form refused: red words on pale red |
| notice | what was asked cannot be had, and what can: brown words on pale yellow |

## Rules

- The full width of the form, above it, with rounded corners.
- A refusal is announced as an alert; a notice as a status.
- One or two sentences: what is wrong, or how many things are; each field says its own reason, C8. (C8)
