An action beside the main one, or one that leads away: Meet the doctors, See this visit, Keep it, Sign out.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| default | beside a C1 or a C3 |
| under the pointer | the pointer over it: a pale green ground |
| large | beside a large C1 on the home page |

## Rules

- White with a green edge and green words, 8px corners.
- Never the only action of a screen.
