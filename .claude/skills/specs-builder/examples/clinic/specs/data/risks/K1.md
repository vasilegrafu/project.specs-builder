Patients' accounts and the visits they booked are not on the new site on its first day

## If it happens

patients who booked on the old site find no visits, phone the front desk, and G1 moves the wrong way; R5 and R8

## Mitigation

the new supplier carries every account and every visit coming up; the practice signs in as 20 test patients the week before launch and compares their visits with the old site's
