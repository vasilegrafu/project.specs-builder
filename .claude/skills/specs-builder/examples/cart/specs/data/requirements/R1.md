When the shop's pages ask for the caller's cart, the cart shall answer the caller's open cart, made empty where there is none, with each line, the count of every unit, the code applied with its percent, and the totals.

## Accepted when

- R1.1 Given a visitor with no cart, when their page asks for the cart, then an empty open cart is answered, with a count of 0 and a total of €0.
- R1.2 Given a cart of 2 Volt 65 chargers and 1 Hush 700, when the page asks for it, then two lines are answered, each with its name, quantity, price when added, price now, whether it is in stock and its total, and the count is 3.
- R1.3 Given a customer signed in, when the page asks for the cart, then the account's cart is answered, not the cart of the session.
- R1.4 Given WELCOME10 applied, when the page asks for the cart, then the answer holds WELCOME10 and 10 percent beside the amount taken off.
- R1.5 Given a line whose product is gone from the catalogue, when the page asks for the cart, then the line is answered by its slug, with no price now and not in stock.
- R1.6 Given the customer's cart was checked out, when their page asks for the cart, then a new empty cart is answered.
