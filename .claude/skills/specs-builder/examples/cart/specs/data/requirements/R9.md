When the nightly job runs, the cart shall abandon every open cart of a customer untouched for thirty days and every open cart of a visitor untouched for 48 hours, and answer how many it abandoned.

## Accepted when

- R9.1 Given a customer's open cart last touched 31 days ago, when the nightly job runs, then it is abandoned and counted.
- R9.2 Given a customer's open cart last touched 29 days ago, when the nightly job runs, then it stays open.
- R9.3 Given a visitor's open cart last touched 49 hours ago, when the nightly job runs, then it is abandoned.
- R9.4 Given a visitor's open cart last touched 47 hours ago, when the nightly job runs, then it stays open.
- R9.5 Given a cart checked out a year ago, when the nightly job runs, then it stays checked out.
