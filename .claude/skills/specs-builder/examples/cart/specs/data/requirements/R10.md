name: Say when a line's price changed
priority: should
serves: G3

While the price now of a line's product differs from its price when added, the cart shall answer the line as changed, with both prices, and keep its total at the price when added.

## Accepted when

- R10.1 Given a line of 1 Hush 700 added at €299.00 and its price now €279.00, when the cart is answered, then the line is marked changed, with €299.00 and €279.00, and its total is €299.00.
- R10.2 Given a line whose price has not changed, when the cart is answered, then the line is not marked changed.
- R10.3 Given a line whose product is gone from the catalogue, when the cart is answered, then the line is not marked changed and has no price now.
