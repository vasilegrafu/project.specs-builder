When accounts signs in a visitor whose cart holds a line, the cart shall carry the visitor's lines into the account's open cart, the larger quantity winning for a product in both, within five of a product and thirty products, and abandon the visitor's cart.

## Accepted when

- R7.1 Given a visitor's cart of 3 Volt 65 chargers and an account's cart of 1 charger and 1 Hush 700, when the visitor signs in, then the account's cart holds 3 chargers and the Hush 700, and the visitor's cart is abandoned.
- R7.2 Given 2 of a product in the visitor's cart and 5 in the account's, when the visitor signs in, then the account's cart holds 5.
- R7.3 Given an account's cart of 29 products and a visitor's cart of 3 others, when the visitor signs in, then the account's cart holds 30 products: the first of the visitor's three is carried, and the other two are not.
- R7.4 Given an account with no open cart, when a visitor with 2 lines signs in, then the account has an open cart of those 2 lines.
- R7.5 Given a visitor's cart with no line, when they sign in, then the account's cart is unchanged.
