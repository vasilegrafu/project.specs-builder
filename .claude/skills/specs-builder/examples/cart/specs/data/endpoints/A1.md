What the cart page and the header's count read: the caller's open cart, its lines and its totals.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1, a visitor, by the session's cookie | read the session's cart | nothing is refused for who calls: a caller with no session is given one, and so a cart (E79) |
| U2, a customer signed in | read the account's cart | -- |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| session | cookie | string: the session's ID | no | the caller's own session: a caller reaches only their own cart, R1 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | always: an empty cart is made where the caller has none open | the cart's ID and status; the count of every unit; each line with its slug, name, quantity, price when added, price now, whether the price changed, whether it is in stock, and its total; the code applied and its percent; the subtotal, the discount, delivery and total; and the sum from which delivery is free |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | a line's product is gone from the catalogue | the line by its slug, with no price now and not in stock |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes, once the first call has made the cart |
| Version | v1, in the path |

## Example

```http
GET /api/v1/cart
Cookie: session=<the shop's session>
```

```http
200 OK
Content-Type: application/json

{"id": "CT-4f1a9c02be", "status": "open", "count": 2, "lines": [{"slug": "volt-65", "name": "Volt 65 charger", "qty": 2, "unit_price": "39.00", "price_now": "39.00", "price_changed": false, "in_stock": true, "total": "78.00"}], "subtotal": "78.00", "discount": "0", "delivery": "0", "total": "78.00", "free_delivery_over": "50.0"}
```

The code applied and its percent are answered beside the amount under a name of their own, as decided (E104); today the amount is written over them (E105), and the example shows a cart with no code.
