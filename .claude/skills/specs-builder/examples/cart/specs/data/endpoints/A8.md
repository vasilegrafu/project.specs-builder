What the nightly job calls: every open cart nobody has touched for its time is abandoned.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U5, the nightly job | let go of the carts nobody touched | no other part calls it |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| now | argument | a moment; the time of the call where left out | no | the moment the thirty days, B7, and the 48 hours, B8, are counted back from, R9 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| returns | always | how many carts it abandoned |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| returns 0 | no open cart is past its time | 0, and nothing changes |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes: a second run at the same moment finds nothing more |
| Version | none: called in the same service until the split |
