What orders calls for the order's total, and what every answer of the cart holds: the subtotal, the discount, delivery and total.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U4, orders, at checkout | read the totals of the cart it closed | no other part calls it, and nothing in the cart stops one: the Boundary holds it |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cart | argument | a cart, open or checked out, N1 | yes | R6 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| returns | always | the subtotal, the discount, delivery and total, in euros to the cent |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| returns | the cart has no line | every total nought, and no delivery |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes: it changes nothing |
| Version | none: called in the same service until the split |
