What orders calls when an order is placed: the cart is closed, and kept as the order's record.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U4, orders, when an order is placed | close the customer's cart | no other part calls it, and nothing in the cart stops one: the Boundary holds it |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cart | argument | the customer's open cart, N1 | yes | it holds a line, and every line is within its stock, B9, R8 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| returns | the cart holds a line and every line is within its stock | the cart, checked out: its lines and totals are the order's record from now on |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| raises OutOfStock | the cart has no line | "the cart is empty" |
| raises OutOfStock | a line is beyond its stock | "nomad-13 is no longer in stock in that quantity" |
| raises CartClosed | the cart is checked out already | "this cart has been checked out; start a new one" |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | no: a second call is refused, the cart being closed |
| Version | none: called in the same service until the split |
