Warm kitchen: cosy and handmade, like walking into the shop. No design system and no brand: the name set as a wordmark. It must never look corporate, cold or like a supermarket. It feels warm, handmade and welcoming, for local families and neighbours ordering a birthday cake. Light only: a cream background, crust brown for text and structure, a raspberry accent, a soft green for success and a deep red for errors. A friendly serif for headings and a clean sans for text, both free under the SIL Open Font License with no reserved name. Roomy, with rounded corners and soft shadows. Drawn illustrations of the bakes as inline SVG, and no photos. Phone first, desktop too, text contrast of at least 4.5:1, with visible keyboard focus.

## Because

The owner chose this direction when asked about the look.

## Instead of

Fresh and light: white, sage and grey with one sans. Old-town craft: deep green, cream and gold with a classic serif.
