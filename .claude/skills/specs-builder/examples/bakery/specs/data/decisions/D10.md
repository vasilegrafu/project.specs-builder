name: What happens after an order
about: N2, R10
---

An order leaves the list and is deleted the day after its pick-up day, collected or not. A customer who rings to cancel is dealt with by phone, and the staff simply do not make the cake.

## Because

The owner asked for the smallest answer; it needs no collected or cancelled status and no extra screen, and it holds nothing for longer than the cake needs.

## Instead of

Collected and cancelled statuses, and keeping past orders.
