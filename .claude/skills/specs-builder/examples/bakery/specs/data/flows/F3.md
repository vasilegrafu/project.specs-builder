A customer finds when the bakery is open and where it is, without ringing.

## Steps

1. From any screen, the customer reads the opening hours, address and phone number in its foot. SC1, R8
2. Or they select Find us and read all seven days on the home page. SC1, R8
3. On a phone, they select the number and ring it. SC1, R8
4. They come to the bakery, outside the website.
