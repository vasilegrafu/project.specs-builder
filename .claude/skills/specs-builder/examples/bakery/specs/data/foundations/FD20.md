name: Accessibility

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Contrast | text at 4.5:1 at the least: --color-surface-dark on --color-surface-page, --color-text-on-action on --color-action, --color-danger on --color-danger-surface, --color-success on --color-success-surface. The lowest of 25 pairs checked is 5.3:1 |
| Focus | a 3px ring of --color-surface-dark, 2px off the element, on every link, button and field; --color-surface-page on the footer |
| Keyboard | Tab reaches every control in reading order; Skip to content comes first of all |
| A screen reader | a refusal is an alert, announced when it appears; a refused field is described by what to fix; a drawing has its words |
| Touch | what can be pressed is 44px high at least |
| Colour | never alone: a status and a refusal say their words |
