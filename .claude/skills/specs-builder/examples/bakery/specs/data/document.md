A small website for Mill Lane Bakery, where customers see what the bakery bakes, order a cake for pick-up, and find its opening hours and address.
The bakery's staff see the orders there and mark them ready.
It is for moving at least 15 of the bakery's 20 or so cake orders a week off the phone, where mistakes happen, and for cutting by half the calls just asking when the bakery is open and where it is.

## Problem

The bakery takes about 20 cake orders a week by phone, usually while the staff are serving at the counter, and that is when mistakes happen.
People also ring just to ask when the bakery is open and where it is.
This is the owner's own account: the 20 is their figure, and the calls have never been counted.

## Users

Customers: people who want to see what the bakery bakes, order a cake for pick-up, or find its opening hours and address (U1). Today they ring the bakery.
Staff: the people who work at the bakery, who see the orders and mark them ready (U2). Today they take cake orders by phone, usually while serving at the counter.

## Journey

1. A customer rings the bakery to order a cake.
2. Someone takes the order by phone, usually while serving at the counter. This is the step that goes wrong: that is when mistakes happen.
3. Separately, someone rings just to ask when the bakery is open and where it is, and someone has to answer.

## Non-goals

- Delivery: a cake is ordered for pick-up at the bakery, so nothing is sent out (D2).
- Ordering anything other than a cake: the owner asked for cakes to be ordered, and everything else the bakery bakes is shown, not ordered (D2).
- Paying on the website: customers pay at pick-up.
- Telling the customer the cake is ready: they come on the day they chose.
- Changing or cancelling an order on the website: they ring the bakery.

## Assumptions

Customers who ring today will order on the website instead. Check: ask the next ten phone customers to order on the website, and count how many do.
Whoever looks after the website can change the list, the hours and the address when the owner asks. Check: name that person before building starts.
Building and hosting the website costs less than it is worth to the bakery. Check: get a price before building starts.
Almost every cake ordered is collected, since customers pay at pick-up. Check: count the cakes not collected in the first month.
Keeping a customer's allergies until the day after pick-up needs no more care than this. Check: ask someone qualified before going live.
