---
id: R5
name: Pick-up days the bakery can do
priority: must
serves: G1
enforces: B1, B2
---

The website shall accept a pick-up day only if the bakery is open on it and it is at least two days after the order is placed.

## Details

The bakery is closed on Mondays.

## Accepted when

- R5.1 Given an order placed on a Thursday, when Saturday is chosen as the pick-up day, then it is accepted.
- R5.2 Given an order placed on a Thursday, when Friday is chosen as the pick-up day, then We need two days' notice. Please choose a later day shows under the day.
- R5.3 Given a Monday, when it is chosen as the pick-up day, then We are closed on Mondays. Please choose another day shows under the day.
