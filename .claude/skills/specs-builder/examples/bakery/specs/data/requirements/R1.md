id: R1
name: See what we bake
priority: must
serves: G1
---

The website shall show what the bakery bakes, each thing with its name, a picture and its price.

## Why

People choose their cake before they order, so this list is where the first goal starts.

## Details

The list comes with the page, and is changed by whoever looks after the website.

## Accepted when

- R1.1 Given the eight things the bakery bakes, three of them cakes, when a customer opens What we bake, then all eight show, each with its name, picture and price.
- R1.2 Given Victoria sponge is a cake and Sourdough loaf is not, when a customer reads the list, then Victoria sponge offers Order this cake and Sourdough loaf offers no way to order it.
