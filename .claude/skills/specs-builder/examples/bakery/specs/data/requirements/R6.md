---
id: R6
name: Say the order is placed
priority: must
serves: G1
---

When an order is recorded, the website shall show the customer its order number, the cake and its size, the pick-up day, the price to pay and where to collect it.

## Accepted when

- R6.1 Given a large Victoria sponge is recorded, when Order placed shows, then it holds the order number, the cake and size, the pick-up day, 38 euros to pay at pick-up and 12 Mill Lane, Little Harbury.
- R6.2 Given Order placed is open, when the customer reloads it, then the same order shows and no second order is recorded.
- R6.3 Given no order has just been placed, when Order placed is opened, then the order form shows.
- R6.4 Given Order placed is open, when the customer reads it, then it says to ring 01632 960123, quoting the order number, to change or cancel the order.
