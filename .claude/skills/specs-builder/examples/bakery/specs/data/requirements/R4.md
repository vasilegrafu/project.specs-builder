---
id: R4
name: Say what needs fixing
priority: must
serves: G1
---

If a customer sends an order with a detail missing or not accepted, then the website shall keep what they entered and say beside each such detail what to fix.

## Accepted when

- R4.1 Given no name is entered, when the customer selects Place order, then Please tell us your name shows under the name and everything entered stays.
- R4.2 Given the phone number is abc, when the customer selects Place order, then Please enter a phone number we can ring shows under the phone number.
- R4.5 Given three details need fixing, when the customer selects Place order, then Please check the 3 details below shows above the form and each says what to fix.
- R4.6 Given words of 31 characters, when the customer selects Place order, then Please keep the words to 30 characters shows under the words; 30 characters are accepted.
