---
id: R9
name: See the orders
priority: must
serves: G1
---

While a member of staff is signed in, the website shall show them the orders, the soonest pick-up day first.

## Accepted when

- R9.1 Given orders for today, tomorrow and Saturday, when staff open the orders, then they show in that order, each with its number, pick-up day, cake, size, words, allergies, customer's name and phone number.
- R9.2 Given one order to do and one ready, when staff open the orders, then each shows under its own heading, To do or Ready.
- R9.3 Given no orders, when staff open the orders, then it says No orders yet.
- R9.4 Given an order placed while the list is open, when staff reload the page, then it shows under To do.
- R9.5 Given the orders cannot be fetched, when staff open them, then it says We could not load the orders, and to reload the page.
- R9.6 Given an order whose pick-up day has passed, when staff open the orders, then it no longer shows, collected or not.
