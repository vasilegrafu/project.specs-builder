---
id: R8
name: Show the hours and address
priority: must
serves: G2
---

The website shall show the bakery's opening hours and address on every screen customers see.

## Why

People ring just to ask when the bakery is open and where it is.

## Accepted when

- R8.1 Given any screen a customer sees, when they read its foot, then it shows the opening hours, 12 Mill Lane, Little Harbury and 01632 960123.
- R8.2 Given the home page, when a customer reads Find us, then it shows all seven days: Monday Closed, Tuesday to Friday 7:30 to 17:30, Saturday 8:00 to 15:00 and Sunday 9:00 to 13:00.
- R8.3 Given a customer on a phone, when they select the phone number, then their phone offers to ring it.
