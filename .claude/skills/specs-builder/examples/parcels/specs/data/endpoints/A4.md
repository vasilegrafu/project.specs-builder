What a shop's site calls to show a customer where the parcel is: every event of the shipment, oldest first.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1 or U2, a key with shipments:read | follow its own account's shipment | 404 for another account's, as for one that does not exist, B10 |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| shipment_id | path | string, PP and twelve digits | yes | the shipment's id, N1 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | the shipment is the account's | its id, its status as it stands, and every event oldest first: when, its kind -- status, scan, attempt or note -- the status where the event changed it, the place, and the line the recipient reads |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | no key, or one not known or revoked | {"detail": {"error": "no API key: send Authorization: Bearer <key>"}} , or that the key is not known or has been revoked |
| 403 | the key lacks shipments:read | {"detail": {"error": "the key lacks the scope shipments:read"}} ; the description lists no 403 here |
| 404 | no shipment has the id, or it is another account's | {"detail": {"error": "no shipment has that id"}} |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes |
| Version | v1, in the path |

## Example

```http
GET /v1/shipments/PP-000000000001/events
Authorization: Bearer <key>
```

```http
200 OK
Content-Type: application/json

{"id": "PP-000000000001", "status": "labelled", "events": [{"at": "2026-10-11T09:02:11Z", "kind": "status", "status": "created", "place": "Parcelpost", "text": "Shipment registered"}, {"at": "2026-10-11T09:02:40Z", "kind": "status", "status": "labelled", "place": "Parcelpost", "text": "Label produced"}]}
```
