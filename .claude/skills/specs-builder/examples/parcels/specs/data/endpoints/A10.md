id: A10
name: A shipment's status changed
kind: event
surface: shipment.status.changed, sent by POST to the subscription's address
shows: R4, R8, R9

---

What Parcelpost sends to a subscriber when a shipment's status changes: its first label, its cancelling, and each move the depot feed makes.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| Parcelpost, to each active subscription of the shipment's account that asked for shipment.status.changed | send one delivery for each change, R8 | a subscription of another account, one paused, or one that did not ask for the event is sent nothing, B8, B10 |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X-Parcelpost-Signature | header | sha256= and the HMAC SHA-256 of the body with the subscription's secret | yes | the subscriber checks it, R8, B11 |
| X-Parcelpost-Event | header | shipment.status.changed | yes | R8 |
| X-Parcelpost-Attempt | header | the try's number, 1 to 6 | yes | B7 |
| event | body | shipment.status.changed | yes | R8 |
| sent_at | body | a time in UTC | yes | R8 |
| shipment | body | the shipment's id, reference, status and service | yes | never an address, R8 |
| last_event | body | the shipment's last event: when, its kind, its place and its text | yes | R4 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 2xx | the subscriber answers within ten seconds | the delivery counts, and the subscription's failures in a row are none again |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| any other | the subscriber answers anything else, or nothing within ten seconds | the try failed: it is tried again after 1 minute, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours, then dropped |
| any other | the tenth failed try in a row | the subscription is paused and sent nothing more |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Timeout | ten seconds a try |
| Tries | six: the first and five more, over 8 hours and 36 minutes, B7 |
| Size | one delivery per subscription and change |

## Example

```http
POST /parcelpost/hooks
Content-Type: application/json
X-Parcelpost-Signature: sha256=<hex>
X-Parcelpost-Event: shipment.status.changed
X-Parcelpost-Attempt: 1

{"event": "shipment.status.changed", "sent_at": "2026-10-11T09:02:41Z", "shipment": {"id": "PP-000000000001", "reference": "VL-1047", "status": "labelled", "service": "standard"}, "last_event": {"at": "2026-10-11T09:02:40Z", "kind": "status", "place": "Parcelpost", "text": "Label produced"}}
```

```http
204 No Content
```
