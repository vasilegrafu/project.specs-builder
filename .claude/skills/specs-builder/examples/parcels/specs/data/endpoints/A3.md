What a shop's program calls to read a shipment as it stands: its status, its label and where its tracking page is.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1 or U2, a key with shipments:read | read its own account's shipment | 404 for another account's, as for one that does not exist, B10 |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| shipment_id | path | string, PP and twelve digits | yes | the shipment's id, N1 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | the shipment is the account's | its id, status, service, reference, when it was made, the label's address or none, the recipient's name, city and country, and the address of its tracking page |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | no key, or one not known or revoked | {"detail": {"error": "no API key: send Authorization: Bearer <key>"}} , or that the key is not known or has been revoked |
| 403 | the key lacks shipments:read | {"detail": {"error": "the key lacks the scope shipments:read"}} ; the description lists no 403 here |
| 404 | no shipment has the id, or it is another account's | {"detail": {"error": "no shipment has that id"}} |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes |
| Version | v1, in the path |
