---
id: A6
name: Cancel a shipment
kind: write
surface: DELETE /v1/shipments/{shipment_id}
shows: R3, R6
about: B6, B9, B10
---

What a shop's program calls when an order is called off before the courier has the parcel: the shipment is cancelled and its label void.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1, a key with shipments:write | cancel its own account's shipment, while created or labelled | 403 for U2, whose key may only read, B6 |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| shipment_id | path | string, PP and twelve digits | yes | a shipment the courier does not yet have, B9 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 204 | the shipment is created or labelled | nothing; the shipment is cancelled, its label void, the event Cancelled by the sender added, and the subscribers told |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | no key, or one not known or revoked | {"detail": {"error": "no API key: send Authorization: Bearer <key>"}} , or that the key is not known or has been revoked |
| 403 | the key lacks shipments:write | {"detail": {"error": "the key lacks the scope shipments:write"}} |
| 404 | no shipment has the id, or it is another account's | {"detail": {"error": "no shipment has that id"}} |
| 409 | the courier has the parcel, or it is already cancelled, delivered or returned | {"detail": {"error": "the shipment is collected; only one not yet collected can be cancelled"}} , with its status; a return is booked with the depot |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | no: a second call is refused with 409 |
| Version | v1, in the path |
