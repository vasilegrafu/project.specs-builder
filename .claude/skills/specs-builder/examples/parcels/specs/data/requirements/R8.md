When a shipment's status changes, or the courier tries to deliver it and cannot, Parcelpost shall send that event to each active subscription of the shipment's account that asked for it, one delivery each, signed with the subscription's secret.

## Accepted when

- R8.1 Given an active subscription to shipment.status.changed, when a shipment's first label is produced, then one POST arrives with the header X-Parcelpost-Event shipment.status.changed, X-Parcelpost-Attempt 1, and a body that says labelled.
- R8.2 Given that delivery, when the subscriber computes the HMAC SHA-256 of the body with its secret, then it equals X-Parcelpost-Signature after sha256=.
- R8.3 Given any delivery, when its body is read, then it holds the shipment's id, reference, status and service and its last event, and no address of the sender or the recipient.
- R8.4 Given a subscription to shipment.delivery.attempted alone, when a label is produced, then nothing is sent to it.
- R8.5 Given a subscription of another account, when this account's shipment changes status, then nothing is sent to it.
- R8.6 Given a shipment just registered, when it is made, then nothing is sent: registering is not a change of status.
