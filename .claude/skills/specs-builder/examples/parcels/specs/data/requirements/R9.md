name: Try again, and pause a subscription that keeps failing

If a subscriber does not answer a delivery with 2xx within ten seconds, then Parcelpost shall try it again after 1 minute, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours, drop it after that, and pause the subscription at its tenth failed try in a row.

## Accepted when

- R9.1 Given a subscriber that answers 500, when a delivery is sent, then it is tried again 1 minute later with X-Parcelpost-Attempt 2.
- R9.2 Given a subscriber that answers nothing within ten seconds, when a delivery is sent, then the try counts as failed.
- R9.3 Given a delivery whose sixth try fails, when it fails, then it is not tried again.
- R9.4 Given a subscription with nine failed tries in a row, when the tenth fails, then it is listed as not active with 10 failures, is sent nothing more, and what happens while it is paused is never sent.
- R9.5 Given a subscription with nine failed tries in a row, when the next try is answered 204, then its failures in a row are 0 and it stays active.
