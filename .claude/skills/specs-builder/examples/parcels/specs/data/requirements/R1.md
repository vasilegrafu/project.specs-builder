When an integrator registers a shipment with a service, a sender, a recipient and a parcel within the limits, Parcelpost shall make it, created, with an id of PP and twelve digits, and answer it with the address of its tracking page.

## Accepted when

- R1.1 Given a key with shipments:write, when a standard shipment of a parcel of 1.2 kg, 40 by 30 by 10 cm, to Leipzig, DE, is registered, then the answer is 201 with an id of PP and twelve digits and the status created.
- R1.2 Given a recipient in the US, when the shipment is registered, then the answer is 422, says that Parcelpost does not deliver to US, and names recipient.country.
- R1.3 Given a parcel of 30 kg, when it is registered, then it is made; given one of 31 kg, the answer is 422 and no shipment is made.
- R1.4 Given a parcel of 120 by 50 by 40 cm, a girth of 300 cm, when it is registered, then it is made; given one of 120 by 50 by 41 cm, the answer is 422 and says the girth is 302 cm and 300 cm is the most.
- R1.5 Given the service economy and a parcel of 25 kg, when it is registered, then the answer is 422, names service and says to choose standard.
- R1.6 Given a key without shipments:write, when a shipment is registered, then the answer is 403 and names shipments:write.
