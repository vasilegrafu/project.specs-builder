# Devices and offline

- **What it is:** Where and on what the product is used. It covers phones, tablets and desktops, an installed app or a web page, and working with a poor connection or none.
- **Applies when:** Always in some degree: every product is opened on some device. It becomes a large area when users are away from a desk, on the move, or somewhere with no signal.
- **If left unsaid:** The product is built for the device the team uses, usually a large desktop screen, and the users turn out to be standing in a warehouse with a phone.
- **Ask:**
  1. Where is the user when they need this: at a desk, walking, in a vehicle, on a site, at home in the evening?
  2. What do they have in their hands at that moment?
  3. Is the connection there reliable?
  4. Do they need it to tell them things when it is closed?
  5. Does it need anything only a phone has: camera, location, scanning, a phone call?
- **Options:**
  - web, desktop first -- one product in the browser, laid out for a large screen; least work, and right when the work is done at a desk.
  - web that works on a phone -- the same product adapting to a small screen; covers occasional use on the move without a second product.
  - an installed mobile app -- best on a phone, with notifications, camera and offline use; a second and third product to build, release through app stores and keep in step.
  - works without a connection -- users carry on offline and changes are reconciled later; essential in the field, and the reconciling is hard.
- **First release:** The web, laid out for whichever device the main journey happens on, and usable on the other. An installed app only when the journey needs what only an app can do. Offline working only when users are provably without signal, and then for the one journey that needs it.
- **Often forgotten:** Which browsers and how old. Small text and small buttons on a phone. A user who starts on one device and continues on another. Old phones and slow connections among the users, not the team. App store review, fees and rules. Two offline users changing the same thing.
- **Becomes:** A devices quality requirement naming devices and browsers; requirements for anything a device must provide; a constraint from an app store; an open question for engineering on offline; non-goals for the platforms left out.
