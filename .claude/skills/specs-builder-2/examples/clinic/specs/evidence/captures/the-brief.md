Please write down what the site does, page by page, so that the new supplier builds the same site.

About 38 visits a month are missed without a word, by the front desk's count for September 2026: a time someone else could have had.

One thing to change: when a patient books from a doctor's page, that doctor should already be chosen in the form. Today they choose again, and some choose the wrong one.

Not now: repeat prescriptions online, and video visits. Both are asked for; both wait for the new appointments system in 2028.

Keep it as it is: cancelling on the site up to 24 hours before a visit; booking up to three months ahead; the reminder the day before; the name and the date of birth changed only at the front desk.

What worries us: that patients' accounts and the visits they have booked are not there on the new site on the first day; and that patients do not notice they can cancel on the site, and go on phoning or not coming.
