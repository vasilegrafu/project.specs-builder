While a visit booked is more than 24 hours away, the site shall let its patient cancel it from its page, after saying what will be cancelled and that it cannot be undone, then free its time and send a note to the patient's email.

## Accepted when

- R6.1 Given LC-2041, booked for 16 October 2026 at 09:30, on 11 October, when Cancel this visit is selected, then the page asks "Cancel visit LC-2041?", says the time will be given to someone else and that this cannot be undone, and offers Cancel the visit and Keep it.
- R6.2 Given that page, when Cancel the visit is selected, then "Visit cancelled" shows, says a note was sent to the patient's email, and LC-2041 shows as cancelled under Past visits.
- R6.3 Given that page, when Keep it is selected, then the visit's page opens again and the visit stays booked.
- R6.4 Given a visit that starts in less than 24 hours, when its page opens, then Cancel this visit is not offered, and the page says to call the clinic.
