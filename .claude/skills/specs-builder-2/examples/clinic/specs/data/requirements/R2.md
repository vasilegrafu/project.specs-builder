When anyone sends the booking form with a doctor, nurse or physiotherapist, a day, one of that one's usual times, what the visit is about and an email, the site shall book the visit, show it with its number, and send its details to that email.

## Accepted when

- R2.1 Given Dr Amara Okafor, 16 October 2026, 09:30, "Blood pressure review" and the patient's email, when Book the visit is selected, then "Your visit is booked" shows the visit's number, LC-2041, with whom, when, what about and where, and says the details were sent to that email.
- R2.2 Given Sam Whitfield chosen, when the Time is opened, then it offers Sam's usual times, 08:00 to 13:40, and no other.
- R2.3 Given 200 characters typed in What is it about?, when one more is typed, then the field takes no more.
- R2.4 Given an email no account has, when the visit is booked, then an account is set up for that email, and the patient is signed in to it.
