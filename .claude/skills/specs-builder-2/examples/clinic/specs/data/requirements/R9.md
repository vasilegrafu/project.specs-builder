---
id: R9
name: Sign out
priority: must
serves: G3
from: S2, signed out, V19
---

When a patient selects Sign out, the site shall sign them out and say so, with a way to sign in again and one to the home page.

## Accepted when

- R9.1 Given a patient signed in, when Sign out is selected, then "You are signed out" shows, with Sign in again and the home page, and the header offers Sign in.
