---
id: R11
name: The clinic's hours, address and privacy
priority: must
serves: G1, G3
from: S2, the home page, the opening hours, contact and privacy, V1, V21, V22 and V23
---

The site shall show the clinic's opening hours, where it is and how to reach it, and what it keeps about a patient, each on a page of its own reached from every page, and the hours of the day it is on the home page.

## Accepted when

- R11.1 Given the opening hours, when Opening hours is opened, then each day of the week shows its hours, Sunday closed, with what to do when the clinic is closed.
- R11.2 Given a Sunday, when the home page opens, then Open today says the clinic is closed; given a Monday, it says 08:00 – 18:30.
- R11.3 Given Contact, when it is opened, then it shows the address, the phone with its hours, the email, how to get there and how to ask for a repeat prescription.
- R11.4 Given Privacy, when it is opened, then it says what is kept, for how long, and which messages are sent.
