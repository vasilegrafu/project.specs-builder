Where a visit stands, in a word on a pale ground: booked, attended or cancelled.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| booked | a visit coming up |
| attended | a visit that has taken place |
| cancelled | a visit cancelled |

## Rules

- Always the word: the colour never says it alone.
- Green for booked, grey for attended, red for cancelled, each on its pale ground, fully round.
