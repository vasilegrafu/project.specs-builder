The one action a screen leads to: Book a visit, Book the visit, Sign in, Save, Book another visit.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| default | where the action can be taken |
| under the pointer | the pointer over it: a darker green |
| keyboard focus | reached with the keyboard: a pale green ring 2 pixels off it |
| large | the home page's first action, beside a large C2 |

## Rules

- Green with white words, 8px corners; never disabled: what is wrong is said when it is selected, C9. (C9)
- Its words are a verb and what it acts on: Book the visit, Book another visit.
- One to a screen, or one to a region; the others are C2, or C3 for what cannot be undone. (C2, C3)
- The header's Book a visit is one too, with white words like the rest (decisions, E52). (E52)
