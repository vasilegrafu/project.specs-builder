---
id: C3
name: Danger button
---

An action that cannot be undone: Cancel this visit, Cancel the visit.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| default | where something can be cancelled |
| under the pointer | the pointer over it: filled red, its words white |

## Rules

- Red words in a red edge on white; filled red only under the pointer.
- The first one opens a page that says what will be lost, SC7; the second, there, does it; a C2 beside it keeps things as they are. (SC7, C2)
