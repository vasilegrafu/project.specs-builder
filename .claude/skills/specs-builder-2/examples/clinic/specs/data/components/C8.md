---
id: C8
name: Text field
---

A field of a form with its label above it, a hint under it where one helps, and its reason under it when it is refused.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| default | a field to fill |
| with a hint | where the field needs a word of why |
| refused | after the form was refused: a red edge, and the reason under it |

## Rules

- The label above the field, in bold; the field the full width of the form.
- A refused field keeps what was typed, and its reason is read with it.
