When orders checks out a cart that holds a line and whose every line is within its stock, the cart shall close it for good, as the order's record.

## Accepted when

- R8.1 Given a cart of 1 Hush 700 with 8 in stock, when orders checks it out, then the cart is checked out and its lines and totals stay as they were.
- R8.2 Given a checked-out cart, when a product is added to it or a code applied, then the cart refuses with "this cart has been checked out; start a new one".
- R8.3 Given an empty cart, when orders checks it out, then the cart refuses with "the cart is empty" and stays open.
- R8.4 Given a line of 2 Nomad 13 and the stock fallen to 1, when orders checks the cart out, then the cart refuses with "nomad-13 is no longer in stock in that quantity" and stays open.
