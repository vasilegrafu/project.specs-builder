When a page adds a product to the cart in a quantity of one to five, the cart shall add it as a line at its price now, or raise the quantity of its line where the product is in the cart already.

## Accepted when

- R2.1 Given an empty cart, when 2 Volt 65 chargers are added, then the cart holds one line of 2 at €39.00 and the count is 2.
- R2.2 Given the cart holds 2 Volt 65 chargers, when 1 more is added, then the line holds 3.
- R2.3 Given the cart holds 4 of a product, when 2 more are added, then the cart refuses with "up to 5 of each product" and is unchanged.
- R2.4 Given the cart holds thirty different products, when a thirty-first is added, then the cart refuses with "a cart holds up to 30 different products".
- R2.5 Given the Nomad 13 with 2 in stock, when 3 are added, then the cart refuses with "only 2 of Nomad 13 left".
- R2.6 Given the Zen 12 with none in stock, when it is added, then the cart refuses with "Zen 12 is out of stock".
- R2.7 Given a slug the catalogue does not have, when it is added, then the cart answers 404 with "no product has that slug".
