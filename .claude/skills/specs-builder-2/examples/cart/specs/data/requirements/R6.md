The cart shall total every cart it answers: the lines added, the code's percent of that taken off and rounded half up to the cent, delivery of €4.90 under €50 after the discount and none from €50 or on an empty cart, and their sum.

## Accepted when

- R6.1 Given one Volt 65 charger at €39.00, when the cart is totalled, then delivery is €4.90 and the total €43.90.
- R6.2 Given two Volt 65 chargers at €78.00, when the cart is totalled, then delivery is €0 and the total €78.00.
- R6.3 Given lines of €52.00 and a code of 10 percent, when the cart is totalled, then €5.20 is taken off, €46.80 is under €50, delivery is €4.90 and the total €51.70.
- R6.4 Given an empty cart, when it is totalled, then every total is €0.
- R6.5 Given a cart orders has checked out, when orders asks its totals, then they are the totals the customer last saw.
