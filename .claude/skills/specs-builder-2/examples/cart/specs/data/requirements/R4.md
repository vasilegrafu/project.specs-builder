---
id: R4
name: Take a product out
priority: must
serves: G3
from: S1, routes.py:80 and service.py:81-85
---

When a page takes a product out of the cart, the cart shall remove its line and answer the cart without it.

## Accepted when

- R4.1 Given a cart of the Hush 700 and a Volt 65 charger, when the Hush 700 is taken out, then the charger's line alone is left and the totals are the charger's.
- R4.2 Given a product that is not in the cart, when it is taken out, then the cart answers as it was, with no refusal.
