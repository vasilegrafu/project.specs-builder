name: Change a line's quantity

When a page sets the quantity of a line, the cart shall set it to that number, from one to five and within the stock, and take the line out where the number is nought.

## Accepted when

- R3.1 Given a line of 1 Hush 700, when its quantity is set to 3, then the line holds 3 and the count rises by 2.
- R3.2 Given a line of the Hush 700, when its quantity is set to 0, then the line is gone.
- R3.3 Given a line of the Nomad 13 with 2 in stock, when its quantity is set to 3, then the cart refuses with "only 2 of Nomad 13 left" and the line is unchanged.
- R3.4 Given a quantity of 6, when it is set, then it is refused and the line is unchanged.
- R3.5 Given a product that is not in the cart, when its quantity is set, then the cart answers 404 with "that product is not in the cart".
