---
id: A4
name: Take a product out
kind: write
surface: DELETE /api/v1/cart/lines/{slug}
shows: R4
about: A1
---

What the cart page's Remove calls: a product's line leaves the cart.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1, a visitor, by the session's cookie | change the session's cart | nothing is refused for who calls: a caller with no session is given one, and so a cart (E79) |
| U2, a customer signed in | change the account's cart | -- |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| slug | path | string: the product's slug | yes | R4 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | always | the cart, as A1 answers it, without the line |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | the product is not in the cart | the cart as it was: nothing is refused |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes: a second call answers the same cart |
| Version | v1, in the path |
