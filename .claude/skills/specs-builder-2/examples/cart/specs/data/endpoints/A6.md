What accounts calls at sign-in: the visitor's lines join the account's cart, and the visitor's cart is abandoned.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U3, accounts, at sign-in | carry a visitor's cart into the account's | no other part calls it, and nothing in the cart stops one: the Boundary holds it |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| visitor_cart | argument | the visitor's open cart, N1 | yes | a visitor's, holding a line, R7 |
| account_cart | argument | the account's open cart, N1, as A9 answers it | yes | R7 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| returns | always | the account's cart, the visitor's lines carried into it: of a product in both, the larger quantity, up to five, B1; none past the thirtieth product, B2; and the visitor's cart abandoned |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| returns | the account's cart holds thirty products already | the account's cart without the visitor's new products; the visitor's cart is abandoned all the same |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | no: called twice, it carries the visitor's lines twice |
| Version | none: called in the same service until the split |
