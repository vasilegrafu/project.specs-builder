What accounts calls at sign-in, and what every route of the cart asks first: the owner's open cart, made where there is none.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U3, accounts, at sign-in | find the visitor's cart and the account's | no other part calls it, and nothing in the cart stops one: the Boundary holds it |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| owner | argument | string: an account's ID, or a visitor's session's ID | yes | whose cart, N5 or a session's, R1 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| returns | always | the owner's open cart; an empty one, made, where the owner has none open |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| returns | the owner's last cart was checked out or abandoned | a new empty cart: a closed cart is never answered |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes, once the first call has made the cart |
| Version | none: called in the same service until the split |
