What the cart page's quantity calls: a line's quantity is set, and nought takes it out.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1, a visitor, by the session's cookie | change the session's cart | nothing is refused for who calls: a caller with no session is given one, and so a cart (E79) |
| U2, a customer signed in | change the account's cart | -- |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| slug | path | string: the product's slug | yes | a product in the cart, R3 |
| qty | body | integer, 0 to 5 | yes | up to five of a product, B1, and within the stock, B9; 0 takes the line out, R3 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | the quantity is within five and the stock, or is 0 | the cart, as A1 answers it, with the line's new quantity, or without the line |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 404 | the product is not in the cart | {"detail": {"error": "that product is not in the cart"}} |
| 409 | more than the stock | {"detail": {"error": "only 2 of Nomad 13 left"}} |
| 422 | qty is missing or not 0 to 5 | the framework's list of what in the body is refused |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes: the same quantity answers the same cart |
| Version | v1, in the path |
