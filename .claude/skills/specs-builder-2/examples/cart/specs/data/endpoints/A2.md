What Add to cart calls: a product and a quantity join the cart, or raise the line already there.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1, a visitor, by the session's cookie | add to the session's cart | nothing is refused for who calls: a caller with no session is given one, and so a cart (E79) |
| U2, a customer signed in | add to the account's cart | -- |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| slug | body | string: the product's slug, as volt-65 | yes | a product of the catalogue, N4, R2 |
| qty | body | integer, 1 to 5; 1 where left out | no | up to five of a product, B1 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | the product is in stock and the line stays within five and the cart within thirty products | the cart, as A1 answers it, with the line added or its quantity raised |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 404 | no product has the slug | {"detail": {"error": "no product has that slug"}} |
| 409 | the product has no stock | {"detail": {"error": "Zen 12 is out of stock"}} |
| 409 | more than the stock is asked for | {"detail": {"error": "only 2 of Nomad 13 left"}} |
| 409 | the line would hold more than five | {"detail": {"error": "up to 5 of each product"}} |
| 409 | a thirty-first product | {"detail": {"error": "a cart holds up to 30 different products"}} |
| 422 | the slug is missing, or qty is not 1 to 5 | the framework's list of what in the body is refused |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | no: each call adds |
| Version | v1, in the path |
