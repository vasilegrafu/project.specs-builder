---
id: R11
name: Only staff see the orders
priority: must
serves: G1
enforces: B4
---

If someone who is not signed in as staff opens the orders, then the website shall ask for the staff password and show no order.

## Details

One password shared by the staff; a sign-in lasts until the browser is closed.

## Accepted when

- R11.1 Given someone not signed in, when they open the orders' address, then the staff password is asked for and no order shows.
- R11.2 Given the right staff password, when it is sent, then the orders show.
- R11.3 Given a wrong staff password, when it is sent, then That password is not right shows and no order shows.
