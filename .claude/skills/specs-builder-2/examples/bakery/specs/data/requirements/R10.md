---
id: R10
name: Mark an order ready
priority: must
serves: G1
depends on: R9
---

When a member of staff marks an order ready, the website shall show it as ready.

## Accepted when

- R10.1 Given an order under To do, when staff select Mark ready, then it moves to Ready.
- R10.3 Given two members of staff with the same order open, when the second selects Mark ready after the first, then it stays ready and nothing fails.
- R10.4 Given the mark cannot be saved, when staff select Mark ready, then the order stays under To do and says That did not save. Try again.
