---
id: R3
name: Order a cake for pick-up
priority: must
serves: G1
enforces: B3
---

When a customer sends an order with a cake, a size, a pick-up day, their name and their phone number, the website shall record it for pick-up at the bakery.

## Why

Orders taken by phone while serving at the counter are when mistakes happen.

## Details

The words on the cake, up to 30 characters, and any allergies are optional. The customer pays at pick-up, so the website takes no payment, and needs no account.

## Accepted when

- R3.1 Given Victoria sponge, large, a pick-up day, Rosa Bell and 07700 900142, when she selects Place order, then the order shows in Orders under To do with those details.
- R3.2 Given an order just sent, when the customer selects Place order a second time, then one order is recorded.
- R3.3 Given the words and allergies are left empty, when the order is sent, then it is recorded without them.
- R3.6 Given Victoria sponge on What we bake, when a customer chooses Order this cake, then the order form opens with Victoria sponge chosen.
- R3.7 Given the form is opened from the menu, when it shows, then no cake is chosen and the list of cakes offers only the three cakes.
- R3.8 Given the order cannot be recorded, when the customer selects Place order, then We could not place your order shows above the button with the bakery's phone number, and what they entered stays.
