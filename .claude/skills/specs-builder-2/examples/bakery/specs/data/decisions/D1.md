Two goals: at least 15 of the 20 or so cake orders a week come through the website, and the calls just asking when the bakery is open and where it is are cut by half, both within six months of going live. Showing what the bakery bakes serves the first: people choose their cake before they order.

## Because

The owner's own words: phone orders are taken while serving at the counter, and that is when mistakes happen.

## Instead of

No goal was turned down; none had been proposed.
