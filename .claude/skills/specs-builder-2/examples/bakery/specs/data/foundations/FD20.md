| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Contrast | text at 4.5:1 at the least: #4a2c17 on #fff8ee, #fffaf3 on #b0244d, #9c1c14 on #fde9e4, #3c6a3d on #e3f0dc. The lowest of 25 pairs checked is 5.3:1 |
| Focus | a 3px ring of #4a2c17, 2px off the element, on every link, button and field; #fff8ee on the footer |
| Keyboard | Tab reaches every control in reading order; Skip to content comes first of all |
| A screen reader | a refusal is an alert, announced when it appears; a refused field is described by what to fix; a drawing has its words |
| Touch | what can be pressed is 44px high at least |
| Colour | never alone: a status and a refusal say their words |
