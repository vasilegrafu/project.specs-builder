name: States and patterns

| Pattern | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Under the pointer | A button deepens, a link deepens, a field's edge darkens, a card lifts. | |
| Keyboard focus | A 3px crust ring, 2px off the element; cream on the footer. | |
| Disabled | A pale ground and muted words, and the pointer says no. | |
| Refused | Says how many details need fixing above the form; each detail says what to fix under itself; what was entered stays. | Please check the 3 details below. |
| Failed | Says what did not happen, that nothing is lost, and what to do next, on the deep red ground. | We could not place your order. |
| Nothing to show | A drawn empty plate, a sentence, and what to expect. | No orders yet. |
| Sending | The button says what it is doing, and cannot be pressed twice. | Placing your order... |
| Done | A title that says it is done, the facts, and how to change it. | Thank you, your order is placed |
| Dates and prices | The day, the date, the month in words; euros with their sign. | Saturday 14 November, from €28 |
