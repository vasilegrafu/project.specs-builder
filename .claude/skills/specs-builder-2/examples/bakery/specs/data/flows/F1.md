A customer sees what the bakery bakes and finds a cake they would like.

## Steps

1. The customer opens the home page and sees the three cakes. SC1, R1
2. They open What we bake and read the whole list, with its prices. SC2, R1
3. A cake they like offers Order this cake, and they go on to order it. SC2, R3
4. Anything that is not a cake is only shown. SC2, R1
