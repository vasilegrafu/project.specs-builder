When a program asks for a shipment or its events, Parcelpost shall answer the shipment's status as it stands and every event it has, oldest first, each with its time, its kind, its place and its text.

## Accepted when

- R4.1 Given a labelled shipment, when its events are asked for, then two are answered, Shipment registered and Label produced, in that order, and the status is labelled.
- R4.2 Given a shipment the depot feed has scanned at a depot, when its events are asked for, then the scan is among them with its place, and the status is as it was before the scan.
- R4.3 Given a key with shipments:read alone, when a shipment of its account is asked for, then it is answered.
- R4.4 Given an id no shipment has, when it is asked for, then the answer is 404 and says that no shipment has that id.
