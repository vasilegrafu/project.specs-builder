---
id: R10
name: Tell the account when a subscription is paused
priority: should
serves: G1
from: S1, the brief
---

When a subscription is paused, Parcelpost shall send an email to the account's technical contact the same day, saying that the subscription is paused.

## Accepted when

- R10.1 Given a subscription paused at 14:10, when it is paused, then the account's technical contact receives an email that day saying that it is paused.
- R10.2 Given a subscription that fails nine tries in a row and then delivers, when the tenth try counts, then no email is sent.
