---
id: A11
name: A delivery was attempted
kind: event
surface: shipment.delivery.attempted, sent by POST to the subscription's address
shows: R4, R8, R9
about: A10, B7, B11
---

What Parcelpost sends to a subscriber when the courier tried to deliver a parcel and could not, so that the shop can tell its customer.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| Parcelpost, to each active subscription of the shipment's account that asked for shipment.delivery.attempted | send one delivery for each attempt not made, R8 | a subscription of another account, one paused, or one that did not ask for the event is sent nothing, B8, B10 |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X-Parcelpost-Signature | header | sha256= and the HMAC SHA-256 of the body with the subscription's secret | yes | the subscriber checks it, R8, B11 |
| X-Parcelpost-Event | header | shipment.delivery.attempted | yes | R8 |
| X-Parcelpost-Attempt | header | the try's number, 1 to 6 | yes | B7 |
| shipment | body | the shipment's id, reference, status and service | yes | never an address, R8 |
| last_event | body | the attempt: when, the kind attempt, the place, and the line the recipient reads | yes | R4 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 2xx | the subscriber answers within ten seconds | the delivery counts |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| any other | the subscriber answers anything else, or nothing within ten seconds | tried again as A10 is, then dropped |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Sent by | nothing in the code read: Q2 asks what sends it |
