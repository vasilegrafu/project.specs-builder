What a shop's program calls for each parcel it sends: the shipment is made, created, with the id the label and the tracking page carry.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1, a key with shipments:write | register a shipment for its own account | 403 for U2, whose key may only read: {"detail": {"error": "the key lacks the scope shipments:write"}} , B6 |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| service | body | string: standard, express, economy | no | standard where none is given; economy up to 20 kg, B5 |
| sender | body | an address: name up to 80 characters, street up to 120, city up to 60, postcode of 3 to 10, country of two letters, each required; phone | yes | who sends it, N1 |
| recipient | body | an address: name up to 80 characters, street up to 120, city up to 60, postcode of 3 to 10, country of two letters, each required; phone | yes | the country one of the nine Parcelpost delivers to, B4 |
| parcel | body | weight_kg, a number over 0; length_cm, width_cm and height_cm, whole numbers over 0; contents up to 200 characters | yes | 30 kg at most, B1; no side over 120 cm, B2; a girth of 300 cm at most, B3 |
| reference | body | string up to 40 characters | no | the sender's own reference, an order number, R5 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 201 | the parcel is within every limit | the shipment: its id, PP and twelve digits; its status, created; its service and reference; when it was made; no label yet; the recipient's name, city and country; the address of its tracking page |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | no key, or one not known or revoked | {"detail": {"error": "no API key: send Authorization: Bearer <key>"}} , or that the key is not known or has been revoked |
| 403 | the key lacks shipments:write | {"detail": {"error": "the key lacks the scope shipments:write"}} |
| 422 | the recipient's country is not served | {"detail": {"error": "Parcelpost does not deliver to US", "field": "recipient.country"}} |
| 422 | the girth is over 300 cm | {"detail": {"error": "the parcel's girth is 302 cm; 300 cm is the most", "field": "parcel"}} |
| 422 | economy and over 20 kg | {"detail": {"error": "economy takes up to 20.0 kg; choose standard", "field": "service"}} |
| 422 | over 30 kg, a side over 120 cm, a field missing or too long | the field and why, as the schema refuses it |

| Idempotent | no: each call that passes makes a shipment |
