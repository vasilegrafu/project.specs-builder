id: A9
name: Stop a subscription
kind: write
surface: DELETE /v1/webhooks/{subscription_id}
shows: R6, R7
about: B6, B10, N2, X3
---

What an integrator calls to stop being told at an address, and to clear a paused subscription before subscribing again.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1, a key with the scope webhooks | stop its own account's subscription, active or paused | 404 for another account's, as for one that does not exist, B10 |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| subscription_id | path | string, WH and eight hexadecimal characters | yes | the subscription's id, N2 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 204 | the subscription is the account's | nothing; it is stopped, and sent nothing more |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | no key, or one not known or revoked | {"detail": {"error": "no API key: send Authorization: Bearer <key>"}} , or that the key is not known or has been revoked |
| 403 | the key lacks the scope webhooks | {"detail": {"error": "the key lacks the scope webhooks"}} ; the description lists no 403 here |
| 404 | no subscription has the id, or it is another account's | {"detail": {"error": "no subscription has that id"}} |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | no: a second call is 404 |
| Version | v1, in the path |
