One thing sold on an invoice: its net price and its category, or none for the standard rate.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning | From |
|---|---|---|
| net price | a Decimal, a whole number or a string of one, never a float and never below zero, B1, B7; summed to the cent |  |
| category | books, food, or medicine in France, where the member state has a reduced rate for it; none for the standard rate |  |
