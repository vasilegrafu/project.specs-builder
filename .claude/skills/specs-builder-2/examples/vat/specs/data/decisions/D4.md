A change of a rate is released on its first day in force, and not before

## Because

the finance team wants it on the invoices from that day (E81); vatkit gives one rate per member state, never one by date, so a release before that day would charge the new rate early, R11

## Instead of

within a month of the change, as the README and the note on the rates say (E3, E69)
