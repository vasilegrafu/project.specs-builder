---
id: R7
name: Check that a VAT number is in its form
priority: should
serves: G1
enforces: B5, B6
from: S1, check_vat_number in numbers.py; S2, the test of a VAT number
---

When a program gives vatkit a VAT number, vatkit shall give it back as it is filed, its two letters and what follows them with no spaces, dots or dashes, where it is in its member state's form, and refuse it where it is not.

## Accepted when

- R7.1 Given de 123 456 789, when it is checked, then DE123456789 is given back.
- R7.2 Given DE12345, when it is checked, then it is refused with "DE12345 is not in the form of a DE VAT number".
- R7.3 Given EL123456789, when it is checked, then it is given back as it is, a Greek number in Greece's form.
- R7.4 Given DE1, when it is checked, then it is refused with "'DE1' is too short to be a VAT number".
- R7.5 Given CH123456789, when it is checked, then it is refused with "CH is not a member state of the EU".
- R7.6 Given PT12, a member state with no form here, when it is checked, then it is given back: only its two letters are checked.
- R7.7 Given any number in its form, when it is checked, then nothing says whether it is registered: that is VIES's to say.
