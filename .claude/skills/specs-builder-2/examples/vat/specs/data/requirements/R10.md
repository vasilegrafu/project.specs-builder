---
id: R10
name: Release each change of a rate on its first day
priority: must
serves: G2
enforces: B8
from: S1, AS_OF in rates.py; S4, 2.3.0
---

When a member state changes a rate, vatkit shall carry the new rate in a release published on the rate's first day in force, whose changelog names the rate and that day.

## Accepted when

- R10.1 Given Finland's standard rate becomes 25.5% from 1 January 2026, when the change is released, then a release published on 1 January 2026 gives 0.255 for FI, the release before it still 0.24, and its changelog says "Finland's standard rate is 25.5%, from 1 January 2026."
- R10.2 Given that release, when a program asks the day its rates hold, then it is 1 January 2026.
- R10.3 Given a release that carries no change of a rate, when it is published, then the day its rates hold stays as it was.
