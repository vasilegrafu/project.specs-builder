id: R5
name: Split a sale by rate
priority: must

When a program gives vatkit a sale's lines, each a net price with its category or none, and the sale's country, vatkit shall give for each rate, highest first and named as an invoice writes it, the sum of its lines, the VAT computed once on that sum, and the gross.

## Accepted when

- R5.1 Given two lines of 10.00 and a line of books of 20.00 in DE, when the sale is split, then 19% holds a net of 20.00, a VAT of 3.80 and a gross of 23.80, and 7% a net of 20.00, a VAT of 1.40 and a gross of 21.40, in that order.
- R5.2 Given a hundred lines of 0.05 in DE, when the sale is split, then 19% holds a VAT of 0.95, computed once on 5.00, not 1.00 computed line by line.
- R5.3 Given a line of 100 in FI, when the sale is split, then its rate is named 25.5%.
- R5.4 Given a line whose category has no reduced rate in that country, when the sale is split, then the whole sale is refused as R1 says, and nothing is given.
