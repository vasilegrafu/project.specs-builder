---
id: R4
name: Give the net in a gross price
priority: must
serves: G1
enforces: B2
from: S1, net in compute.py; S2, the test of the net
---

When a program asks for the net in a gross price in a member state, vatkit shall give the gross divided by one plus the rate, to the cent, so that the VAT in that price is the gross less the net.

## Accepted when

- R4.1 Given 119.00 in DE, when the net is asked, then it is 100.00, and the VAT in the price is 19.00.
- R4.2 Given 119 of books in DE, when the net is asked, then it is 111.21.
- R4.3 Given 0.03 in DE, when the net is asked, then it is 0.03, and the VAT in the price is 0.00: the net and the VAT make the gross to the cent.
- R4.4 Given a country, a category or an amount that R1 or R8 refuses, when the net is asked, then it is refused in the same words.
