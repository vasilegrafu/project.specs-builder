What a person at the shell runs to see whether a customer's VAT number is in its form, and how it is filed.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U2, U3: anyone at a shell where vatkit is installed | check any VAT number; never whether it is registered, B6 | nothing is refused for who runs it: vatkit has no keys and no accounts |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| number | argument | a VAT number, quoted where it holds spaces | yes | as A8 takes it, B5, R7 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| prints and ends with 0 | the number is in its form, or its member state has no form here | the number as it is filed: DE123456789 |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| prints to the error stream and ends with 2 | the number is too short, out of its form, or not a member state's | "vatkit: DE12345 is not in the form of a DE VAT number" |
