id: A8
name: Check that a VAT number is in its form
kind: read
surface: vatkit.check_vat_number(number)
shows: R7
about: B5
---

What a program calls before it writes a business's VAT number on an invoice: whether the number is in its member state's form, and the number as it is filed.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1, U3: any program that imports vatkit | check any VAT number; never whether it is registered, B6 | nothing is refused for who calls: vatkit has no keys and no accounts |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| number | argument | string: its two letters and the rest, with spaces, dots or dashes or none: "de 123 456 789" | yes | four characters at least; the two letters a member state's, EL for GR, B5; the rest in that state's form where vatkit has one, R7 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| returns | the number is in its member state's form, or that state has no form here | the number as it is filed: "DE123456789" |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| raises BadVatNumber | under four characters | "'DE1' is too short to be a VAT number" |
| raises BadVatNumber | the rest is out of its member state's form | "DE12345 is not in the form of a DE VAT number" |
| raises UnknownCountry | the two letters are not a member state's | "CH is not a member state of the EU" |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Version | the major version changes only when this function's name or what it takes changes (E70) |
| Network | none: whether the number is registered is VIES's to say, and vatkit never asks, B6 |
