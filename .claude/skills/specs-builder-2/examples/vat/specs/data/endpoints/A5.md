name: Split a sale by rate
kind: read
surface: vatkit.split(lines, country)
shows: R1, R5, R8

---

What a program calls for an invoice's figures: a sale of many lines, by rate, with the VAT computed once on each rate's sum.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1, U3: any program that imports vatkit | split any sale of one member state | nothing is refused for who calls: vatkit has no keys and no accounts |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| lines | argument | pairs of a net price and a category or None: [("10.00", None), ("20.00", "books")] | yes | each net price as A2 takes it, B1, B7; each category as A1 takes it, B4, R5 |
| country | argument | string: a member state's two letters in either case | yes | a member state, EL for GR, B5, R1 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| returns | every line is taken | for each rate, highest first and named as an invoice writes it, "19%" or "25.5%": its net, the sum of its lines; its VAT, computed once on that sum, B3; its gross |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| raises BadAmount | a line's net price is a float, is not a number, or is below zero | "0.1: give a Decimal, a whole number or a string, never a float" |
| raises UnknownCountry | the country is not a member state, or none | "CH is not a member state of the EU" |
| raises UnknownCategory | the member state has no reduced rate for a line's category, B4; the whole sale is refused, never a part of it | "DK has no reduced rate for books" |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Version | the major version changes only when this function's name or what it takes changes (E70) |
| Idempotent | yes: the same call answers the same within one release |
