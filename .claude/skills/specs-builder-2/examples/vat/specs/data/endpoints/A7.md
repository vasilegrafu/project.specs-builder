---
id: A7
name: Round an amount to the cent
kind: read
surface: vatkit.round_cents(value)
shows: R2, R8
about: B1, B2, B7
---

What a program calls to take an amount to the cent as vatkit does every one: half a cent and more goes up.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U1, U3: any program that imports vatkit | round any amount | nothing is refused for who calls: vatkit has no keys and no accounts |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| value | argument | a Decimal, a whole number or a string of one: "0.005" | yes | never a float, never below zero, B1, B7, R8 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| returns | the amount is taken | a Decimal to the cent, half up: 0.01 for 0.005 |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| raises BadAmount | the amount is a float, is not a number, or is below zero | "'-1' is below zero: a refund is a sale of its own, written as a credit" |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Version | the major version changes only when this function's name or what it takes changes (E70) |
| Idempotent | yes: the same call answers the same within one release |
