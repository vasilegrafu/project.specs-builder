The operator on call, told by the supplier of a stock take, reads what it would change before letting it through for one run.

## Diagram

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    start(["The supplier announces a stock take, or the guard refused a feed"]) --> open["The operator opens the feed"]
    open --> real{"A stock take the supplier announced?"}
    real -->|"no: a feed cut short"| call(["Call the supplier's desk; nothing is written"])
    real -->|"yes"| dry["A dry run: the report of what would change, and that the guard would refuse it"]
    dry --> read{"Does the report look like the stock take?"}
    read -->|"no"| call
    read -->|"yes"| lift["The guard lifted for one run, by hand, as the runbook says"]
    lift --> run["A run writes the stock take"]
    run --> back(["The guard put back; the shop's stock matches the feed"])
```

## Steps

1. The supplier announces a stock take, or the morning mail says more than half the SKUs would have changed. R4, R6
2. The operator opens the feed; a feed cut short is the supplier's to mend, and nothing is written.
3. The operator runs a dry run: the report of every change is printed, with a line that says the guard would refuse it, and nothing is written or mailed. A2, R8
4. Where the report looks like the stock take, the operator lifts the guard for one run as the runbook says, and runs the job by hand. A1, R4
5. The run writes the stock take, keeps its report and mails it; the operator puts the guard back. A1, R5, R6

## When it goes wrong

| What fails | Then | Seen by | Stands on |
|---|---|---|---|
| the dry run's feed is out of shape or stale | the reason is printed and nothing is mailed, R8 | the operator, at the command line | R8 |
| the guard is not put back after the stock take | the next night's broken feed goes through, R4 | nobody, until the shop's stock is wrong: the runbook says to put it back | R4 |
| the catalogue refuses a write of the stock take | stopped with exit 3, the writes before it kept, R5 | the operator, by the exit code and the mail; the rerun writes what still differs | R5 |
