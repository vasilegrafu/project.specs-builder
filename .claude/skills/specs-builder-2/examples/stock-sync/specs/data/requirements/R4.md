---
id: R4
name: Refuse a change too large to be real
priority: must
serves: G1
enforces: B2
from: S2, stock_sync.py, the comparison, and its test; S3, the runbook's "Changed too much"
---

If more than half of the shop's SKUs would change at once, then the stock sync shall refuse the run and change nothing.

## Accepted when

- R4.1 Given a shop of 1,000 SKUs and a feed that changes 501 of them, when the two are compared, then the run is refused with "501 of 1000 SKUs would change: more than 50%", ends with exit 2, and no SKU changes.
- R4.2 Given a feed that changes 500 of the 1,000, when the two are compared, then the run goes on and writes the 500.
- R4.3 Given the guard refused a feed, when the morning comes, then the mail says how many would have changed of how many, R6.
