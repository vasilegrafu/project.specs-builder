id: R5
name: Bring the shop's stock in line with the feed
priority: must
serves: G1, G3
enforces: B7, B9, B10
from: S2, stock_sync.py, the comparison and the writes, and its tests; S3, the runbook's exits 0, 1 and 3; S4, the log of 10 October
---

When the feed is read and passes its checks, the stock sync shall set each SKU the shop sells whose quantity differs from the feed's to the feed's quantity, and leave every other SKU as it is.

## Accepted when

- R5.1 Given the shop holds A at 1 and B at 5, and the feed says A 1 and B 2, when the run writes, then B is 2, A is untouched, and the run ends with "done: 1 SKU(s) changed" and exit 0.
- R5.2 Given a SKU of the feed the shop does not sell, when the two are compared, then it is passed over, and the log counts it.
- R5.3 Given a SKU the shop sells that the feed does not carry, when the two are compared, then its quantity stays the shop's own, R11.
- R5.4 Given a SKU listed twice, at 40 and then 38, when the feed is read, then 38 is the feed's quantity and the log names the line of the second.
- R5.5 Given nothing differs, when the two are compared, then nothing is written and the run ends with "nothing to do" and exit 1.
- R5.6 Given the catalogue refuses the write of the 9th of 23 SKUs, when it refuses, then the run stops with exit 3 naming that SKU, the 8 before it stay written, and the 14 after it are not written.
- R5.7 Given a run stopped so, when the operator reruns it, then only what still differs is written.
