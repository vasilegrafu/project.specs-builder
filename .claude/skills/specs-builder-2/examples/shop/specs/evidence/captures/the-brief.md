# The owner's brief, 2026-10-11

- **Given:** 2026-10-11, by Jonas, the owner of Voltline, pasted into the conversation
- **Redacted:** nothing: the brief names no customer

We are moving Voltline to a new platform in the spring of 2027, and the shop must behave on the new one as it does today. Nothing is to be added for the move; nothing is to be lost. Please write down what the shop does, screen by screen and call by call, so that the new team builds the same shop.

What goes wrong today: every week about 12 orders are placed by phone because something on the site stopped the customer, most often at the checkout. 68 of every 100 checkouts started are abandoned, by the shop's own counter, September 2026. The new platform is meant to fix the slowness behind that, not to change what the customer sees.

What should be better afterwards: orders placed by phone, under 2 a week; checkouts abandoned, under 55 of 100, by June 2027.

Who uses it: visitors who browse and search; customers with an account, who buy, see their orders and keep their details; and the two of us in the warehouse, Lea and me, who move the orders along as admin.

Not now: a wishlist, reviews written on the site, gift cards. They come up every year and every year we say no; the move is not the moment.

Keep it as it is: the catalogue of six categories, four products to a page; the cart with its code box; free delivery from 50 euros; the three ways to pay; the order statuses as the terms page has them.

The API description the shop serves at /api/openapi.json is written by the server from its routes; it is the shop, not someone's notes about it.
