id: C8
name: Refusal
---

What a form says above itself when it is refused: what is wrong, or how many things are.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| default | after a form was refused |

## Rules

- Red words on pale red, rounded, the full width of the form, above its first field.
- Announced when it appears: it has the role of an alert.
- One sentence: what is wrong, or how many things need a look; each field says its own reason, C7. (C7)
