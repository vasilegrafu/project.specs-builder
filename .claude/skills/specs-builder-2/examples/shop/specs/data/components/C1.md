---
id: C1
name: Primary button
---

The one action a screen leads to: Add to cart, Checkout, Place order, Sign in. A screen has one, or one per card.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| default | where the action can be taken |
| under the pointer | the pointer over it: a darker blue |
| keyboard focus | reached with the keyboard: a blue ring 2 pixels off it |
| disabled | an action that cannot be taken now: a product out of stock |

## Rules

- Its words are a verb and what it acts on: Add to cart, Place order.
- One to a screen, or one to a card; the others are C2. (C2)
- Disabled only where the reason shows beside it, as Out of stock does; otherwise it is enabled, and what is wrong is said when it is selected.
