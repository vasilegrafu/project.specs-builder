What a product costs, and, for a deal, what it cost before.

## Variants

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| default | a product at its price |
| a deal | a product under its old price, while its stock at that price lasts (B8) |

## Rules

- The price first and in bold; the old price after it, struck out, in grey; then Deal on red.
- Euros with the sign before and two decimals: €1,099.00.
