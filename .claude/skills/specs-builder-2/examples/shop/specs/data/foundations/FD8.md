name: Patterns

| Pattern | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A refused form | Says what is wrong, or how many things are, above the form, C8; each field says its own reason under itself, C7; what was typed is kept. | Three things need a look. |
| An action that cannot be taken | Its button is disabled, and the reason shows beside it. | Out of stock beside a disabled Add to cart |
| Nothing to show | Says so in a sentence, and offers the way on. | Your cart is empty. Start shopping |
| A list longer than a page | Four products to a page, with C12 under it and where the list stands above it. | Showing 1–4 of 6 products |
| A page the user may not see | One sentence that says it is not theirs, and a way back. | This part of the shop is for its staff. |
