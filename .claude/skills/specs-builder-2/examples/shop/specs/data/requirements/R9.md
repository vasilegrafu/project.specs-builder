id: R9
name: Recover the password
priority: must
serves: G2
enforces: B7
from: S2, the password page and the page after, V15 and V16
---

When a visitor sends their email on the password page, the shop shall send a link to choose a new password where an account has that email, and say the same on the page after whether or not one does.

## Accepted when

- R9.1 Given an account's email, when Send the link is selected, then the page says "If an account has that address, a link is on its way. It works for one hour." and the email holds a link.
- R9.2 Given an email no account has, when Send the link is selected, then the page says the same and nothing is sent.
- R9.3 Given a link an hour old, when it is opened, then it no longer works.
