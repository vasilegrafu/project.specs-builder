---
id: R6
name: Check out and place an order
priority: must
serves: G1, G3
enforces: B5
from: S2, the checkout, V22; S3, POST /orders
---

When a customer selects Checkout with a cart that holds a line, the shop shall ask for the delivery address and one of three ways to pay, show the total, and on Place order make the order from the cart, placed.

## Accepted when

- R6.1 Given a signed-in customer with a cart, when Checkout is selected, then the checkout shows the name, the street and number, the city and the postcode, each required, and Card, PayPal and Invoice with Card chosen.
- R6.2 Given the address filled and a way to pay chosen, when Place order is selected, then an order is made with the cart's lines, the address and the total, in the status placed, and the cart is emptied.
- R6.3 Given a required field empty, when Place order is selected, then the field is marked and no order is made.
- R6.4 Given a line out of stock since it was added, when Place order is selected, then the shop refuses the order and says which line.
- R6.5 Given a visitor, when Checkout is selected, then sign in comes first, and the checkout after it with the same cart.
