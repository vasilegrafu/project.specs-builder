---
id: R3
name: Search the products
priority: must
serves: G2
from: S2, the search's results, V8 and V9; S3, GET /products with q
---

When anyone types words in the search and sends it, the shop shall list the products whose name or description holds the words, say how many match, and say when nothing does.

## Accepted when

- R3.1 Given the word "headphones", when the search is sent, then the page says "5 products match." and lists the five as a category does.
- R3.2 Given words no product holds, when the search is sent, then the page says "Nothing matches." and "Check the spelling, try fewer words, or browse a category.", and offers the popular searches.
- R3.3 Given the home page, when a popular search is selected, then the search's results for that word open.
