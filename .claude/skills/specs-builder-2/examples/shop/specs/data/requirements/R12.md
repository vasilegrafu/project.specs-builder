id: R12
name: Follow my orders
priority: must
serves: G1, G2
enforces: B3, B9
from: S2, my orders and two of them, V24, V25 and V34; S3, GET /orders and GET /orders/{number}
---

When a customer opens My orders, the shop shall list their orders with the number, date, count of items, total and status, and open each to its lines, its steps from placed to delivered and its delivery address, with Cancel this order while it is still placed.

## Accepted when

- R12.1 Given a customer with four orders, when My orders opens, then the four are listed, each leading to its page.
- R12.2 Given a placed order, when it opens, then the four steps show Placed as the one reached and Cancel this order is offered.
- R12.3 Given a shipped order, when it opens, then Shipped is the step reached and no Cancel is offered.
- R12.4 Given another customer's order number, when its address is opened, then the shop answers as for an order that does not exist.
