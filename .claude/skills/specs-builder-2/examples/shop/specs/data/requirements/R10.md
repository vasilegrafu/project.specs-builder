---
id: R10
name: Sign out
priority: must
serves: G2
from: S2, the account menu and the page after signing out, V21 and V32; S3, DELETE /session
---

When a customer selects Sign out in the account menu, the shop shall end their session, keep their cart for the next sign-in, and say so.

## Accepted when

- R10.1 Given a signed-in customer, when Sign out is selected, then the page says "You are signed out" and "Your cart is kept for the next time you sign in.", and the header shows Sign in and Sign up.
- R10.2 Given a cart of two lines at sign-out, when the customer signs in again, then the cart holds the two lines.
