---
id: R7
name: Sign in
priority: must
serves: G2
from: S2, sign in and its refusal, V11 and V12; S3, POST /session
---

When a visitor sends their email and password, the shop shall sign them in to their account, keep them signed in on the device where they ask it, and refuse a wrong pair above the form with both fields marked and what was typed kept.

## Accepted when

- R7.1 Given an account's email and password, when Sign in is selected, then the customer is signed in and the header greets them by name.
- R7.2 Given a wrong email or password, when Sign in is selected, then the page says "The email or the password is wrong." above the form, both fields are marked, and what was typed stays.
- R7.3 Given Keep me signed in ticked, when the browser is closed and opened again, then the customer is still signed in.
