---
id: R5
name: Change the cart
priority: must
serves: G2, G3
enforces: B1, B2
from: S2, the cart, V10 and V21; S3, GET /cart
---

When a visitor or a customer opens the cart, the shop shall show each line with its product, price, quantity and total, let them change a quantity or remove a line, take a discount code, and show the subtotal, the delivery and the total.

## Accepted when

- R5.1 Given a cart of two lines, when it opens, then each line shows the product, its price, a quantity with a minus and a plus, its total and Remove, and the summary shows the subtotal, the delivery and the total.
- R5.2 Given a line of quantity 1, when its minus is selected, then the line is removed.
- R5.3 Given a subtotal of €377.00, when the cart opens, then the delivery is "Free"; given a subtotal of €39.00, then the delivery is €4.90.
- R5.4 Given a code, when Apply is selected, then the summary shows what the code takes off, or the field says that the code is not known.
- R5.5 Given an empty cart, when it opens, then the page says "Your cart is empty." and offers to start shopping.
