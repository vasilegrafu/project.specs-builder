---
id: R1
name: Browse a category
priority: must
serves: G2
from: S2, the category page, V3 and V4; S3, GET /products
---

When a visitor opens a category, the shop shall list its products four to a page, sorted by one of five orders, with filters by price, stock and rating beside the list and numbered pages under it.

## Accepted when

- R1.1 Given a category of six products, when it is opened, then four products are listed with their name, stars, count of reviews, price, stock state and Add to cart, the page says "Showing 1–4 of 6 products", and page 2 holds the other two.
- R1.2 Given the list, when a sort order is chosen and applied, then the same products are listed in that order.
- R1.3 Given the list, when a page's number, Previous or Next is selected, then that page opens and is marked as the one open.
- R1.4 Given a filter ticked and applied, when the list is shown again, then only the products that match it are listed.
