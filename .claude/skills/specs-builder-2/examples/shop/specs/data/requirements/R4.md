---
id: R4
name: Add a product to the cart
priority: must
serves: G2, G3
enforces: B1, B5
from: S2, the product page, V5 and V7; S3, POST /cart/lines
---

When a visitor or a customer selects Add to cart on a product in stock, the shop shall add it to their cart in the quantity chosen, one to five, or raise the line's quantity where the product is in the cart already.

## Accepted when

- R4.1 Given a product in stock and a quantity of 2, when Add to cart is selected, then the cart holds a line of that product with quantity 2 and the header's count rises by 2.
- R4.2 Given the product is in the cart with quantity 4, when 2 more are added, then the shop refuses it with "up to five of each product" and the cart is unchanged.
- R4.3 Given a product out of stock, when the cart is asked to add it, then the shop refuses it and the cart is unchanged.
- R4.4 Given a visitor's cart, when they sign in, then the cart is theirs under the account.
