id: R2
name: See a product
priority: must
serves: G2
enforces: B5, B8
from: S2, the product page, V5, V6 and V7; S3, GET /products/{slug}
---

When a visitor opens a product, the shop shall show its name, its stars and count of reviews, its SKU, its price with the old price and a Deal mark where it has one, its stock state, a line about it, its specifications, its reviews, the three promises and three more products.

## Accepted when

- R2.1 Given a product with a deal, when its page opens, then the price, the old price struck out and the word Deal show together.
- R2.2 Given a product with three or fewer left, when its page opens, then the low-stock words show in place of "In stock".
- R2.3 Given a product out of stock, when its page opens, then "Out of stock" shows, Add to cart is disabled, and the page offers to tell the customer when it is back.
- R2.4 Given an address of a product that does not exist, when it is opened, then the shop answers that no product has that slug.
