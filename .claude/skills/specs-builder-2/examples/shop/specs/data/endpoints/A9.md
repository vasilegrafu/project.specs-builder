What the sign-in page calls: the email and the password, and whether to keep the session.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| anyone, U1 | sign in to an account of theirs | nothing is refused but a wrong pair |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| email | body | string, an email | yes | the account's email, N4 |
| password | body | string | yes | the account's password, N4 |
| keep | body | boolean | no | Keep me signed in: the session outlives the browser, R7 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 204 | the pair is right | nothing: the session cookie is set, and the visitor's cart joins the account's |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | the email or the password is wrong | {"error": "the email or the password is wrong"} , which the page shows above the form |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Rate | the shop's own: 10 tries a minute per address, then 429 |
| Idempotent | yes: signing in twice is one session |
| Version | v1, in the path |
