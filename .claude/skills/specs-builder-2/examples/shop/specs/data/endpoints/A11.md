name: Create an account

What Create account calls: the name, the email, the password and the terms accepted make an account, signed in.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| anyone, U1 | make an account with an email no account has | a field wrong is refused with 422 |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| name | body | string | yes | the customer's name, N4 |
| email | body | string, an email | yes | an email, and one no account has, N4 |
| password | body | string, at least 8 characters | yes | eight characters with a number, B6 |
| terms | body | boolean | yes | the terms accepted, R8 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 201 | every field passes | the account, signed in: the session cookie is set |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 422 | a field is refused: which, and why, one message per field | {"errors": {"email": "That is not an email address.", "password": "At least 8 characters, with a number."}} , which the page shows under each field |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | no: the same email a second time is refused as taken |
| Version | v1, in the path |
