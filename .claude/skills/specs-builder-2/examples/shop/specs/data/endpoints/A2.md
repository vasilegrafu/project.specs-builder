What a product page shows: one product by its slug.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| anyone: U1, U2, U3 | read the product | nothing is refused |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| slug | path | string: aero-14 | yes | the product's slug, N1 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | a product has the slug | the product: its name, category, price and old price, stock state, rating, reviews, SKU, blurb, specifications and picture |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 404 | no product has the slug | {"error": "no product has that slug"} , which the shop shows as its page for an address it does not have |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes |
| Version | v1, in the path |
