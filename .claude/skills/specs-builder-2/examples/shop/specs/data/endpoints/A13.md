What Save changes calls: the profile's fields, changed.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U2 or U3, signed in | change their own profile | 401 signed out |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| name | body | string | no | the customer's name, N4 |
| email | body | string, an email | no | an email no other account has, N4 |
| phone | body | string | no | N4 |
| address | body | an address: name, street, city, zip | no | the delivery address, N4 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | every field given passes | the profile, changed |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | not signed in | {"error": "not signed in"} |
| 422 | a field is refused | {"errors": {"email": "That is not an email address."}} , which the page shows under the field |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes: the same change twice leaves the same profile |
| Version | v1, in the path |
