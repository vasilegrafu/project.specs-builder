What an order's page reads: one order of the signed-in customer, or any order for the admin.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U2, signed in | read an order of their own | 404 for another customer's, as for one that does not exist, B9 |
| U3, the admin | read any order, with its customer | 401 signed out |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| number | path | string, VL-1042 | yes | the order's number, N3 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | the order is the caller's, or the caller is the admin | the order: its number, date, status, lines, total and address |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | not signed in | {"error": "not signed in"} |
| 404 | no order has the number, or it is another customer's | {"error": "no order has that number"} , which the shop shows as its page for an address it does not have |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes |
| Version | v1, in the path |
