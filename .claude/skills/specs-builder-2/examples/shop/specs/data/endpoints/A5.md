What My orders reads: the signed-in customer's orders, newest first.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U2 or U3, signed in | read their own orders | 401 signed out |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| session | header | the session cookie | yes | the caller's own session; the orders are theirs alone, B9 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | signed in | the orders, newest first, each with its number, date, status, lines, total and address |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | not signed in | {"error": "not signed in"} |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | yes |
| Version | v1, in the path |
