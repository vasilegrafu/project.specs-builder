---
id: A4
name: Add a product to the cart
kind: write
surface: POST /api/v1/cart/lines
shows: R4
about: B1, B5, N1
---

What Add to cart calls: a product and a quantity join the cart, or raise the line already there.

## Who may call

| Caller | May | Else |
|---|---|---|
| U2 or U3, signed in | add to their own cart | 401 signed out |

## Takes

| Name | Where | Type | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| slug | body | string: the product's slug | yes | a product of the catalogue, N1 |
| qty | body | integer, 1 to 5 | yes | up to five of a product, B1 |

## Answers

| Status | When | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | the product is in stock and the line stays within five | the cart, with the line added or its quantity raised |

## Fails

| Status | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | not signed in | {"error": "not signed in"} |
| 409 | the product is out of stock, B5, or the quantity is over five | {"error": "the product is out of stock, or the quantity is over five"} , which the page shows beside Add to cart |

## Limits

| What | Is |
|---|---|
| Idempotent | no: each call adds |
| Version | v1, in the path |
