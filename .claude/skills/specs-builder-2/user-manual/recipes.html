specs-builder-2 user manual
Part 5: Recipes

I want to...

Start from the question you have. Each recipe gives the quickest way, in the application and on the command line.

The recipes

I want to knowIn the applicationOn the command line
Why does this exist?Open the node, and read Links: what it serves and enforces. Follow them up to a goal.spec.py trace R4
Its reasons, its findings with their quotes, and what shows it.
Where did this come from?Read Linked from, then open a finding and read its Quote.spec.py trace B1
The same, as text.
What would this change reach?Press Impact under the title. Click a node to go a step further.spec.py impact R4
What goes stale at once, and what may follow.
What is still not settled?Open What is open, from the overview or the Document menu.spec.py open
The same list.
May we agree it?Read What is open from the top.spec.py ready
Every reason it may not be agreed.
What's this ID?Pause on it for its kind. Right click it for its card.spec.py node R4
The node and its links, both ways.
What does this word mean?Search it, and open the Term.spec.py kinds term
What a term holds.
Who may do this?Open the requirement, and read may be done by under Linked from.spec.py node R4
Look for the roles' lines.
Who changed this, when and why?Open the node, and read History at its foot.spec.py history B1
Each change, by whom, and why.
How are these two things joined?In the Matrix, use the path icon, then choose the two ends.It prefers links a change passes along, so it says why one bears on the other.
What has no link at all?In the Matrix, choose No links in the list under Find a node.A good way to find what was forgotten.
Is anything here unseen in the product?Under What is open, read Documented only and To investigate.spec.py open
Each item says how to find out.
How do I show this to someone?Print the page, or press Export.spec.py export
One HTML file of everything.
How do I ask for a change?Press Request a change on the page, or just say it in Claude Code.spec.py inbox
Lists what's waiting.
What should I build first?This is one for the command line.spec.py plan
The order to build in, foundations first.
What has no test yet?Open What is built, once there's code.spec.py coverage
It exits 1 while a must or a should has no test.
