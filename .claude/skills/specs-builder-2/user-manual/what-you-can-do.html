What you can do with it

Now you have the idea, here's what you can actually use it for.

Five jobs it does

Describe something newSay what you want in your own words, or be asked. You get requirements, screens and working mock-ups.Three ways to start

A reviewerCheck what's claimed against what was seen

What you get out

  • A small website on your own computer that you open with a double click. Nothing goes online.
  • One file of the whole thing, to send to someone who has nothing installed.
  • Any page on a sheet of paper, or as a PDF.
  • Working mock-ups of every screen, at phone, tablet and desktop size.
  • A list of checks and an order to build in, for whoever builds it.

When not to use it

It takes more thought up front than a page of notes. For a one-week experiment with nobody to hand it to, notes are fine. It earns its keep when more than one person is involved, when the product will change, or when an AI will build from it.
